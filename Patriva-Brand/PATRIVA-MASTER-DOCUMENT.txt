SECTION 1 - THE BIG IDEA
==============================================================================

Patriva is a secure digital VAULT for the documents and decisions a family
cannot afford to lose - a will, powers of attorney, guardianship, insurance,
property, accounts, passwords, medical directives, funeral wishes, and personal
messages. Patriva keeps them safe while you are alive, keeps them current as
life changes, and delivers them to the people you trust at the exact moment
they need them.

THE LINE THAT SAYS EVERYTHING (draft - alternates in Section 8):

   "Protected today. In the right hands when it matters."

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE:
When something happens to a person - death or sudden incapacity - their family
is often left searching. Where is the will? Who is the lawyer? What accounts
exist? What were their wishes? The information exists, but it is scattered,
locked, or known only to the person who is now gone. That gap turns an already
painful moment into weeks of stress, cost, and conflict.

WHAT PATRIVA DOES:
Patriva closes that gap. Everything important lives in one secure vault, kept
current, and released to the right people at the right time - verified,
private, and legally sound.

THE PIVOT, IN ONE SENTENCE:
We are moving from "a group of Islamic family brands" to "one universal Vault,
entered first through the Muslim community, then opened to everyone."

SECTION 2 - WHAT THE VAULT IS (AND IS NOT)
==============================================================================

This definition matters more than anything else in the document. If we get it
wrong, we sound like free cloud storage. If we get it right, we are a category.

THE VAULT IS NOT:
  - Not just file storage (Google Drive and Dropbox are free).
  - Not just a will generator (a will is one document among many).
  - Not a religious product (that is Muslim Will, one door into the Vault).

THE VAULT IS THREE THINGS FREE STORAGE CAN NEVER BE:

  1. GUIDED - It does not just hold documents; it helps you CREATE the
     important ones properly (starting with a complete, legally valid will and
     Powers of Attorney) so the vault is actually filled with the right things.

  2. CONTROLLED RELEASE - This is the heart of the product. You name trusted
     people (family, executor, "keyholders"). The Vault releases the right
     information to the right person only when a defined trigger occurs - your
     death, your incapacity, or a rule you set - after verification. Nothing
     leaks; nothing is lost.

  3. LIVING & TRUSTED - It reminds you to keep things current, is encrypted,
     Canadian-hosted, and built so a family can trust it with their whole life.

WHAT A PERSON KEEPS IN THE VAULT (examples):
  - Will, Powers of Attorney (property & personal care), guardianship
  - Insurance policies, property deeds, a list of financial accounts
  - Passwords and digital assets
  - Medical directives and health wishes
  - Funeral / burial wishes
  - Personal letters and messages to loved ones

THE EMOTIONAL CORE:
"If something happens to me, my family will not be lost. Everything they need
is in one place - and it will reach them safely, when they need it."

SECTION 3 - THE STRATEGY: WIN A NICHE, THEN GO MAINSTREAM
==============================================================================

We do NOT try to sell a vault to everyone on day one. We use the strongest play
in business: win one specific, underserved, high-trust market deeply - then
expand the same product outward.

  STEP 1 - THE WEDGE (now): Muslim Will.
     A live, trusted, revenue-generating product for an underserved community.
     Every Muslim Will is delivered INTO the Patriva Vault. We prove the Vault
     with people who already trust us, at low acquisition cost (mosques, imams,
     community, word of mouth).

  STEP 2 - DEEPEN (next): open the Vault subscription to that same community.
     Not just a will - keep all your important documents current and
     release-ready. Turn one-time will buyers into ongoing members.

  STEP 3 - GO WIDE (future): open the Vault to the general market.
     The Vault has nothing religious in its core, so anyone can use it. We
     offer it to the mainstream (with a general will-builder alongside Muslim
     Will), and to businesses (employers, advisors, charities) who want to give
     it to their people.

WHY THIS ORDER WINS:
  - The niche gives us trust, low CAC, and real revenue while we perfect the product.
  - The Vault architecture is universal, so going mainstream is a marketing and
    market decision - not a rebuild.
  - Muslim Will keeps converting the whole time; nothing is put at risk.

SECTION 6 - THE BUSINESS MODEL
==============================================================================

VALUE PROPOSITION:
The trusted vault that makes sure your family is never left searching - your
most important documents and wishes, created properly, kept current, and
released to the right people at the right moment. Islamically correct and
legally valid where it needs to be; universal underneath.

HOW WE MAKE MONEY (REVENUE STREAMS):

  STREAM A - GUIDED DOCUMENT FEES (one-time)  [LIVE via Muslim Will]
    A flat, transparent, upfront price for a complete, guided package. The live
    Muslim Will package already includes: Islamic Last Will, minor guardianship,
    Power of Attorney for Property, Power of Attorney for Personal Care,
    qualified Islamic scholar review, provincial legal workflow (Ontario first),
    and the secure Vault. Future: a general (non-Islamic) will-builder feeds the
    same Vault.

  STREAM B - THE VAULT SUBSCRIPTION (recurring - THE ENGINE)  [next]
    A monthly or annual membership to keep the vault stored, current,
    release-ready, and supported. Recurring revenue is native to a vault - you
    keep it because your life is in it. This is the long-term value driver and
    it works for Muslim and non-Muslim customers identically.

  STREAM C - B2B / SPONSORED ACCESS  [can start now]
    - Sponsored Seats: charities, zakat funds, and foundations PAY to fund will
      + vault access for families who cannot afford it. Bulk, mission-led,
      recurring.
    - Charity Partnerships: co-branded will-and-vault pages; donors are guided
      to include the charity within the Islamic one-third bequest.
    - Later: white-label / employer / advisor / insurer distribution of the
      Vault to their people.

  STREAM D - EXPANSION REVENUE  [grows over time]
    Family / multi-user plans, more document types, add-on guided products,
    upgraded storage or support tiers.

WHY THIS MODEL IS STRONG:
  - RECURRING BY NATURE: a vault is a subscription people keep for life.
  - HIGH SWITCHING COST / MOAT: once your family's whole life is in the vault,
    you do not leave. Controlled release + trust + legal validity are very hard
    to copy.
  - LOW ACQUISITION COST: the Muslim community gives us warm, trusted, cheap
    entry while we perfect the product.
  - HUGE ADDRESSABLE MARKET: everyone has important documents and everyone
    faces death or incapacity. The niche is the door; the world is the room.
  - MISSION-ALIGNED: sponsored seats let us serve families who cannot pay while
    partners fund the impact.
