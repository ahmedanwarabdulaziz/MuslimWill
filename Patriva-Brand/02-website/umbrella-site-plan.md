# Patriva — Standalone Umbrella Website Plan

> A standalone Patriva site whose job is to be **the first visible doorway into the ecosystem** — explain the umbrella, show the milestone map, introduce the four brands, and route each visitor into the right service. This is a thinking draft; let's shape it before any code.

## 1. The job of this website

The homepage must answer the **new first question**:

> "Why should I trust Patriva to support my family across important life milestones?"

Not the old one ("why trust Muslim Will for wills"). Muslim Will becomes one strong proof of a bigger platform.

**Three things the site must do, in order:**
1. Make a family feel *seen* at a milestone (lead with the moment).
2. Build trust in Patriva as the container (the elder who's present at every milestone).
3. Route the visitor cleanly into the right service brand — without burying Muslim Will under four equal products.

## 3. Homepage narrative (top to bottom)

Each block below = one scroll section. Copy shown is draft, in-voice.

**① Hero**
- Headline: **With you at every milestone. Not just the difficult ones.**
- Sub: *Patriva is the Islamic family services group that walks with Muslim families — from your first happy commitment to your final farewell, and every milestone in between.*
- Primary CTA: "Find your milestone" (scrolls to milestone map) · Secondary: "Explore the Family Plan"
- Tone: warm, calm, a family/joy image — **not** death-adjacent imagery.

**② The shift / promise**
- One short paragraph: most family services only appear when something goes wrong. We're present at the wedding, the birth, the new home — and yes, the hard moments too.

**③ The milestone map** (the centrepiece)
- Interactive row/journey of life milestones; clicking a milestone reveals the service + message. This is the heart of the page.

|-----------|---------|---------|
| Engagement | Mizan | "You said yes. Now make it official — the Islamic way." |
| Marriage | Mizan | "Start your marriage with clarity, love, and barakah." |

**④ Meet the family of services**
- Four cards (Muslim Will, Mizan, Sakina, Ikram), each: name, milestone, one-line promise, "Learn more."
- **Visual weight note:** per the strategy, do NOT give all four equal weight on day one. Muslim Will (live, proven) and Mizan (happiest entry) lead; Sakina/Ikram can be present but lighter, or "growing." **[decide]**

**⑤ The Family Plan**
- Framed as *membership and belonging*, not features. "One plan. Every milestone. Complete peace of mind."
- Three-step framing from the framework: acknowledge what they've done → open the gap → present the plan as one complete picture (not three products).

**⑥ Trust / why Patriva**
- Community-first proof: rooted in the Muslim community in Canada, Islamically grounded + legally valid, real people. Founder's voice (Ahmed).

**⑦ Soft close**
- Repeat the line: *With you at every milestone. Not just the difficult ones.*
- CTA: "Start with your will today" (the strongest immediate revenue path) + "Book a conversation."

## 4. Navigation & conversion logic

- Even on the umbrella site, **Muslim Will stays the clearest immediate conversion path** — it's the live, proven revenue engine. Lead the *story* with happy milestones; make the *easiest button* "start your will."
- Every service page ends with its natural cross-sell (per framework): Muslim Will → Mizan + Ikram; Mizan → Muslim Will → Ikram; Sakina → Mizan.

## 5. Look & feel (starting hypotheses — to design in /assets)

- **Mood:** calm, warm, premium, dignified. Closer to a trusted advisory/healthcare feel than a flashy startup.
- **Imagery:** real family milestones — wedding rings, a newborn's hand, house keys, an elder's hands. Joy first; never lead with grief imagery.
- **Colour/typography:** TBD — **[decide]** do we have brand colours/logo, or design here?
- **Inspiration to study, not copy:** FreeWill's clarity and guided flow — but Patriva is premium, Muslim-specific, and milestone-led, not free/broad-market.

## 6. Build approach (phasing the actual rollout)

Even though we're designing the *full standalone umbrella vision*, I'd stage the build so we don't launch four unexplained brands at once:

- **Phase 1:** Patriva home + Muslim Will (live) + Mizan (happiest entry) + Family Plan teaser. Sakina/Ikram present as "growing / join the waitlist."
- **Phase 2:** Full Sakina + Ikram service pages, full multi-brand nav, Family Plan checkout.

This protects conversion clarity and lets us test whether Patriva *increases* trust or creates confusion before going all-in.
