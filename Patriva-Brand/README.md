# Patriva-Brand — our thinking space

This folder is where we (Ahmed + Claude) think through **Patriva's persona and online presence together**, before anything gets built into the live site.

It is intentionally separate from the Next.js codebase. Nothing here ships automatically — these are working documents we shape, argue with, and refine.

## What Patriva is (the foundation we're building on)

Patriva is the **Islamic Family Services Group** — a master brand that is *with Muslim families at every milestone, not just the difficult ones.*

| Brand | Milestone it owns | One-line role |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Patriva** | the whole journey | Master/trust brand, the doorway |
| **Muslim Will** | estate planning | Islamic wills — wasiyya, guardians, assets |
| **Mizan** | marriage & agreements | Mahr, marriage contracts, mediation, family constitution |
| **Sakina** | wellbeing & hard seasons | Counselling, imam referral, psychologist network |
| **Ikram** | the farewell | Burial coverage, ghusl, kafan, reception, coordination |

**Master line:** *"With you at every milestone. Not just the difficult ones."*

## Open decisions (let's resolve these as we go)

1. **Visual identity** — Do we have a logo / colours / fonts for Patriva yet, or do we design that here too?

4. **The monthly Family Plan** — Is the subscription live at launch, or introduced after the individual services prove out?
5. **Geography** — Canada-first confirmed. Any specific provinces/cities to lead with?
