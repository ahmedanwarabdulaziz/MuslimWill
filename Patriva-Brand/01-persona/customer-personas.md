# Patriva — Customer Personas

> Five families we serve. Each is tied to a milestone and a natural entry brand. These are drafts — tell me where they don't match the real community you know.

The point of personas here isn't demographics for their own sake. It's so that every page, post, and flyer can be written *to a specific person at a specific moment* — which is exactly how the framework says we should lead: **with the milestone, not the product.**

---

## Persona 1 — "The Newlyweds" (entry: Mizan)

**Who:** Bilal (29) & Aisha (27), engaged, planning a nikah in 6 months. Canadian-born or long-settled, both working, modern but want their marriage done *properly* in the Islamic sense.

**Moment:** Engagement → marriage. The happiest, highest-trust entry point into the whole platform.

**What they're feeling:** Excited, a little overwhelmed by logistics, unsure how to handle mahr and a contract "the right way" without it feeling transactional or unromantic.

**What they don't know they need yet:** A will the moment they marry; guardianship the moment they have a child.

**How we show up:** *"Start your marriage with clarity, love, and barakah."* Make the paperwork feel like part of the celebration, not bureaucracy.

**Cross-sell path:** Mizan (marriage contract) → Muslim Will → Ikram.

---

## Persona 2 — "The New Parents" (entry: Mizan → Muslim Will)

**Who:** Yusuf (34) & Maryam (31), just had their first (or second) child.

**Moment:** A new person to protect. The single most motivating trigger for estate and guardianship planning.

**What they're feeling:** A deep new sense of responsibility. Fear of "what happens to our child if something happens to us" — but we must never *lead* with that fear.

**How we show up:** *"You have a new person to protect. Make sure they always are."* Frame it as the natural, loving thing a parent does — not a morbid task.

**Cross-sell path:** Guardianship + will update → annual review → Family Plan.

---

## Persona 3 — "The Established Provider" (entry: Muslim Will)

**Who:** Tariq (45), homeowner, possibly a small-business owner, two or three kids, aging parents. The financial anchor of an extended family.

**Moment:** Estate planning, business milestone, first home — anything where what he's *built* now needs protecting.

**What they're feeling:** Quiet awareness that he should "sort things out" and keep putting it off. Wants it done correctly per Sharia *and* valid under Canadian law.

**How we show up:** *"Your wishes, written. Your family, protected. Your amanah, fulfilled."* Emphasise that Islamic correctness and legal validity are both handled.

**Cross-sell path:** Muslim Will → Mizan (family constitution / business) → Ikram → Family Plan. This persona is the strongest revenue anchor.

---

## Persona 4 — "The Caring Elder / Parent" (entry: Ikram, or buying for others)

**Who:** Fatima (60+), or an adult child arranging for an aging parent. Thinking about the farewell, and about not being a burden.

**Moment:** Later life; or a family quietly preparing for the farewell of a loved one.

**What they're feeling:** A wish for dignity and for the family not to scramble at the hardest moment. In grief, they need everything *handled*.

**How we show up:** *"Every farewell, handled with honour. Every family, cared for."* Calm, dignified, logistics-off-your-shoulders.

**Cross-sell path:** Ikram is most often the *downstream* pairing from Muslim Will. Also a respectful standalone entry for elders.

---

## Persona 5 — "The Family in a Hard Season" (entry: Sakina)

**Who:** Any family hitting stress — marital strain, intergenerational conflict, a dispute over money or property.

**Moment:** Difficulty, disagreement, a season that needs support or mediation.

**What they're feeling:** Isolation, shame about needing help, reluctance to "air" problems outside the family.

**How we show up:** *"Every family goes through hard seasons. You don't have to face it alone."* Zero judgment. Confidential. Community-rooted (imam + psychologist network).

**Cross-sell path:** Sakina → Mizan (to document a resolution/mediation outcome). Also an upstream doorway into the whole platform.

## How the personas connect (the milestone journey)

A single family moves through most of these over a lifetime — which is the entire Patriva thesis:

```
Engagement ─▶ Marriage ─▶ New child ─▶ First home ─▶ Building/parenting
   Mizan        Mizan       Mizan+MW      Mizan+MW         MW
        │                                                    │
        └──────────────  (hard seasons) Sakina  ◀───────────┘
                                   │
                            Estate planning (MW) ─▶ The farewell (Ikram)
```

The **Family Plan** subscription is how we say to a family: *you don't have to buy these one crisis at a time — be a member, and we're with you the whole way.*
