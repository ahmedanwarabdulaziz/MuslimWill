# BHOPE x Muslim Will Growth Strategy

Prepared: April 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

BHOPE already has a strong foundation for a high-converting legacy-planning campaign. The offer is emotionally compelling, the causes are meaningful, and the partnership with Muslim Will is strategically sound. The main gap is not the idea itself. The main gap is packaging.

Humaniti feels more effective because it presents itself as a complete trust ecosystem: a movement, a mission, a set of values, clear programs, clear calls to action, and practical tools that make the user feel confident immediately. By comparison, the current BHOPE and BHOPE x Muslim Will pages feel more like campaign pages than a permanent category-leading platform.

The strategic opportunity is clear:

- BHOPE should own the trust, mission, and impact narrative.
- Muslim Will should own the legal, planning, and compliance pathway.
- Together, they should build a permanent Islamic legacy-planning category, not just a short-term promotion.

This is not only a marketing opportunity. It is also a long-term donor development and legacy fundraising opportunity.

---

## What Makes Humaniti More Effective

Humaniti performs strongly because it combines emotional appeal with structural clarity. Its website creates confidence quickly and makes action easy.

### Humaniti's strongest advantages

- Clear movement-based positioning
- Strong values language such as trust, transparency, collaboration, and excellence
- Immediate action paths tied to concrete causes
- Tools and educational content that reduce hesitation
- Consistent trust signals across the site
- A tone that empowers rather than pressures

### The lesson for BHOPE

BHOPE does not need to imitate Humaniti's style exactly. It needs to adopt the same strategic discipline:

- make the mission unmistakable
- make the user journey simple
- make the causes concrete
- make trust visible
- make the offer feel permanent and institutionally credible

---

## Core Diagnosis of the Current BHOPE x Muslim Will Offer

The current direction is promising, especially the sponsored-will model, but several issues reduce conversion and long-term search visibility.

### What is already working

- The family-first positioning is strong
- The sponsored offer is more compelling than a discount model
- The connection to sadaqah jariyah is emotionally powerful
- BHOPE's campaign areas are strong and easy to understand
- Dr. Yasser is a major trust asset for community adoption

### What is limiting effectiveness

- The pages feel campaign-based rather than category-defining
- The offer still contains ambiguity around eligibility, bequest structure, and terms
- The access path creates friction before trust is fully earned
- The relationship between BHOPE and Muslim Will is not yet architected as cleanly as it should be
- Technical SEO foundations are incomplete on the co-branded pages
- AEO readiness is partial because the content is not yet organized as a full answer hub

## What BHOPE Needs To Be More Effective

## 1. Build a permanent Legacy Giving hub

BHOPE should not treat Islamic wills as a one-page campaign. It should create a permanent section of the website such as:

`Legacy Giving`

or

`Islamic Will & Legacy Planning`

This section should include:

- main landing page
- educational pillar pages
- FAQs
- trust and compliance page
- causes and legacy allocation page
- webinar/events page

This turns BHOPE from a campaign promoter into an authority in Muslim legacy planning.

## 2. Lead with empowerment, not fear

The strongest messaging frame is:

`Protect your family today. Leave a legacy of hope tomorrow.`

The site should focus more on:

- clarity
- responsibility
- dignity
- family care
- intentional giving

and less on:

- uncertainty
- legal confusion
- fear of dying without documents

The fear-based case can exist, but it should support the message, not dominate it.

## 3. Make trust impossible to miss

BHOPE should visibly present:

- registered charity number
- mission and values
- impact areas
- who prepares the will
- who reviews Islamic considerations
- who does not provide legal advice
- privacy and consent standards
- whether the bequest is optional or required

Users should not need to infer credibility. It should be visible within seconds.

## 4. Connect legacy planning to real BHOPE impact

Humaniti succeeds because users quickly understand where their giving goes. BHOPE should do the same by linking charitable bequests directly to:

- Healing Hands
- Bright Futures
- Hope in Crisis

Each cause should have:

- a short impact explanation
- sample outcomes
- imagery
- a deeper linked page

This makes legacy giving feel real and meaningful.

## 5. Use Dr. Yasser as a strategic bridge

The Dr. Yasser page is more than an about page. It is a trust-conversion asset.

BHOPE should use him in:

- webinar invitations
- launch videos
- partner outreach
- mosque toolkit materials
- landing-page trust sections
- community leader introductions

He helps translate the offer from a transactional product into a trusted community pathway.

## Best Way To Inject Muslim Will Into BHOPE

The right model is not "BHOPE sending traffic away." The right model is "BHOPE hosting a trusted pathway powered by Muslim Will."

### Recommended positioning

`BHOPE Legacy Giving, powered by Muslim Will`

or

`BHOPE Islamic Will Pathway, in partnership with Muslim Will`

### Recommended architecture on BHOPE

#### A. Add a permanent navigation item

Use one of:

- Islamic Will
- Legacy Giving
- Plan Your Legacy

#### B. Create a layered landing structure

The landing page should flow in this order:

1. Why this matters for Muslim families in Canada
2. How BHOPE helps
3. How Muslim Will delivers the planning pathway
4. What the participant receives
5. How charitable legacy options work
6. FAQs
7. Clear CTA

#### C. Keep two pathways

Do not rely on one sponsored path only.

Use:

- `Fully Sponsored Will Program` for eligible BHOPE members and approved supporters
- `Partner Access Will Pathway` for users who are not eligible for sponsorship

This avoids losing valuable users who are interested but not yet qualified.

#### D. Present Muslim Will as the engine, not the hero

Inside BHOPE, Muslim Will should appear as:

- the guided platform
- the legal and process partner
- the compliance and review pathway

BHOPE should remain the primary trust brand on the BHOPE site.

#### E. Add stronger process proof

Show:

- how the process works
- approximate time required
- where Islamic review fits
- where legal review fits
- what happens at signing
- what is included in the package

This reduces hesitation and increases qualified intent.

## Best Way To Inject BHOPE Into Muslim Will

Muslim Will should not overload its generic product pages with one charity partnership. Instead, it should add BHOPE in a structured, scalable way.

### Recommended model

Create a `Charity Partners` or `Legacy Partners` section on Muslim Will.

### Recommended BHOPE placement

#### A. Featured partner page

Create a BHOPE-specific page that explains:

- who BHOPE is
- why BHOPE is a trusted partner
- what causes a user can support
- what the sponsored or partner offer includes
- how the pathway works

#### B. Add BHOPE at the charitable legacy step

The sequence on Muslim Will should be:

1. protect family
2. document guardianship and wishes
3. structure inheritance properly
4. optionally or conditionally include charitable legacy

This keeps family responsibility primary and makes the giving step feel principled, not forced.

#### C. Use BHOPE as an impact option, not just a logo

Where BHOPE appears, users should see:

- causes
- outcomes
- trust signals
- why BHOPE is relevant

#### D. Link to BHOPE for mission depth

Muslim Will should link out to:

- BHOPE campaigns
- BHOPE about page
- Dr. Yasser's page when appropriate

This creates authority and trust transfer in both directions.

## AEO Strategy

Current Google guidance indicates that AI visibility comes primarily from strong SEO fundamentals, crawlable content, clear answers, and trust-rich pages, not from special "AI tricks."

For BHOPE and Muslim Will, AEO should mean:

- clear question-based headings
- concise direct answers near the top of each page
- strong authorship and reviewer signals
- structured site architecture
- accurate schema
- helpful, people-first content

### Recommended answer-engine content patterns

Use exact user-style questions such as:

- How do I make an Islamic will in Canada?
- Can Muslims leave sadaqah jariyah in their will?
- What happens if I die without a will in Canada as a Muslim?
- How do I protect guardianship for my children in a will?
- What is the Islamic 1/3 bequest rule?

Each page should answer the question immediately in the first paragraph, then expand with practical detail.

---

## Technical SEO Priorities

Based on the page review, the main technical gaps on the co-branded files are:

- missing canonical tags
- missing JSON-LD structured data
- incomplete metadata on some pages
- no visible sitemap strategy in the project files

### Recommended technical actions

#### For every key page

- set a unique title tag
- add a high-quality meta description
- add a canonical URL
- add a robots meta tag only if there is a special need
- ensure descriptive image alt text

#### Add structured data

Priority order:

1. `Organization`
2. `BreadcrumbList`
3. `Person` for trusted experts where appropriate
4. `Article` for educational pieces

FAQ content should still exist for user clarity, but it should not be relied on as the main rich-result strategy.

#### Submit crawl and indexing signals

- generate an XML sitemap
- submit it in Google Search Console
- use IndexNow for Bing and supporting engines
- request recrawl for high-priority pages after launch

## Business and Marketing Recommendations

## 1. Treat this as legacy fundraising infrastructure

The sponsored model should be viewed as a long-term donor development channel, not a one-time subsidy.

It creates a ladder:

- donor
- recurring donor
- legacy interest
- will completion
- annual review

That makes the program strategically valuable even before bequests are realized.

## 2. Launch a mosque and community partner toolkit

Create a reusable outreach package for:

- masajid
- community leaders
- nonprofit partners
- fundraisers
- Islamic speakers

Toolkit assets should include:

- one-page explainer
- webinar deck
- short FAQ
- imam talking points
- WhatsApp copy
- email copy
- social carousel assets

## 3. Run education-first campaigns

Do not rely only on "start now" calls to action.

Use:

- webinars
- short reels
- FAQ videos
- downloadable legacy checklist
- family planning guides

This improves trust and converts colder audiences over time.

## 4. Publish an annual Legacy Impact Report

This is one of the best ways to make BHOPE feel more institutionally mature, similar to Humaniti.

Include:

- number of completed wills
- number of legacy commitments where appropriate and consented
- supported cause areas
- educational reach
- stories and testimonials
- privacy-safe aggregate reporting

## 5. Build a consent-based legacy CRM flow

Track:

- lead source
- webinar attendance
- page visits
- eligibility status
- intake started
- intake completed
- review completed
- annual reminder status

Avoid collecting more estate-sensitive information than is necessary.
