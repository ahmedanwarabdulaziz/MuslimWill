## 2. The Detailed Version (For the Inner Page / FAQ)
*This version dives into the legal protections. It explicitly points out that traditional law firms charge extra for these documents, positioning Muslim Will as an incredible value for money.*

**What Exactly is Included in Your Muslim Will Package?**

A proper Islamic estate plan goes far beyond a simple will. True peace of mind means ensuring your family is protected whether you pass away or simply become medically incapacitated.

Many traditional law firms or online generators will charge you a base rate for a simple will, and then charge you hundreds of dollars extra to add your "Powers of Attorney" (your protection while you are alive). 

At Muslim Will, we believe a family isn't protected until *all* scenarios are covered. That is why our standard package includes four distinct, legally-binding documents at no hidden extra cost:

### Included Document 1: The Islamic Last Will & Testament
* **When it takes effect:** After you pass away.
* **What it does:** Legally enforces the Islamic rules of inheritance (Mawarith) in a Canadian court. It allows you to dedicate up to 1/3 of your estate to charity (Wasiyyah), forgive debts, and outline burial instructions so your family isn't forced to guess what you would have wanted.

### Included Document 2: Minor Guardianship Assignment
* **When it takes effect:** After you pass away (if your children are underage).
* **What it does:** If you do not legally name a guardian, the Canadian government may place your children in temporary foster care while a judge decides who gets custody. This document ensures your children immediately go to the guardian you trust to raise them upon Islamic principles.

### Included Document 3: Continuing Power of Attorney for Property
* **When it takes effect:** While you are still alive, but medically incapacitated (e.g., severe illness, coma, accident).
* **What it does:** If you are incapacitated, your spouse or family cannot automatically access your bank accounts or sell your assets to pay for your care. This document legally authorizes someone you trust to manage your money, pay your bills, and protect your wealth while you recover.

### Included Document 4: Power of Attorney for Personal Care
* **When it takes effect:** While you are still alive, but unable to communicate.
* **What it does:** Gives a trusted family member the legal authority to make critical healthcare, surgery, and life-support decisions on your behalf, ensuring your medical treatment aligns with Islamic ethical guidelines.
