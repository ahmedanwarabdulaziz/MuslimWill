# Muslim Will Workflow Copy

## 1. The Short Version (For the Homepage)
*This version is concise and scannable, designed to build trust quickly without overwhelming the user.*

**A Guided Pathway from Family Details to Legal Execution**

1. **Intake & Details:** Select your plan and use our secure platform to provide all your family information, guardian choices, and necessary documents. 
2. **Dual Expert Review:** Your documents undergo a rigorous two-step review process—first by qualified Islamic scholars for inheritance considerations, then by our Legal Team for Ontario legal validity. **We keep you informed with automated email updates at every stage of the review, so you never have to wonder about the status of your file.**
3. **The Pre-Attestation Briefing:** A 1-on-1 preparation call with our follow-up team to review exactly what you need for the final signing and ensure everything goes smoothly.
4. **Final Attestation & Delivery:** Attend a guided final Zoom attestation session where the required parties complete the signing and witnessing process according to Ontario remote execution requirements. Once properly signed and witnessed, your completed package is delivered with the relevant final documents.

## 2. The Detailed Version (For the "How It Works" / "Our Process" Inner Page)
*This version elaborates on the full 8-step internal operational workflow. It builds massive trust by showing the complete concierge-level machinery behind the service, and explicitly mentions the communication touchpoints so the client never feels left in the dark.*

**Our Complete, Concierge-Level Will Process**
We believe that preparing your final wishes should never be left to an automated template. Here is exactly what happens when you start your journey with Muslim Will:

### Phase 1: Your Details
* **Step 1: Registration & Plan Selection** – Create your private account and select the subscription plan that best fits your family's needs.
* **Step 2: Intake & Uploads** – At your own pace, complete our guided intake form and upload any required supporting documents. *(You will receive an immediate confirmation email once your file is securely submitted and actively queued for Scholar Review).*

### Phase 2: The Dual-Review Process (With Email Tracking)
* **Step 3: Islamic Scholar Review** – Within 3 working days, our scholars review your intake to ensure all bequests and guardianship directives align perfectly with Islamic principles. If adjustments are needed, we contact you directly. *(Once approved, you receive an automated email letting you know your file has passed Sharia compliance and is now under legal review).*
* **Step 4: Independent Legal Review** – Upon scholar approval, our legal professionals spend the next 3 working days reviewing your documents against applicable Ontario legal requirements. *(Once finalized, you receive an email prompting you to schedule your Pre-Attestation Briefing).*

### Phase 3: Preparation and Attestation
* **Step 5: The Pre-Attestation Briefing** – Once your documents are finalized, our team schedules a dedicated preparation call with you. We will walk you through the final signing process, confirm witness requirements, and schedule your final attestation meeting so there are no surprises.
* **Step 6: The Final Zoom Attestation** – In the final Zoom attestation session, our process is designed to follow Ontario remote execution requirements, including proper witness coordination, wet-signature/counterpart handling where applicable, and the involvement of a required Ontario legal professional/licensee.

### Phase 4: Completion
* **Step 7: Official Delivery** – You receive your finalized, legally binding, and faith-aligned Will, complete with all official stamps.
