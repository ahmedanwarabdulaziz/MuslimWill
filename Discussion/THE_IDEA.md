# The Idea

## Working Title

`Muslim Vault`

This document captures the new direction for Muslim Will so we can discuss it, refine it, and use it as the base for the next phase.

---

## 1. Why We Need This Change

The current market is showing strong resistance to a direct `$350` will offer.

Main reasons:

- free websites already exist
- some Islamic centers offer wills for free or for any donation
- users compare Muslim Will only as a will-creation service
- this makes the offer feel expensive before the user understands the deeper value

Because of that, the business needs to move away from competing only on:

- "a will for $350"

and toward a stronger model based on:

- ongoing value
- private family guidance
- timed access
- secure storage
- post-death and incapacity clarity
- recurring subscriptions

---

## 2. Core Direction

The new direction is:

- keep the `$350` package
- reposition it as the `premium package`
- introduce a lower-friction subscription offer called `Muslim Vault`
- create a tiered structure with free, starter, and premium options

This creates three levels:

### A. Free Tier

Entry-level access for awareness and lead generation.

### B. Starter Subscription

Ongoing monthly subscription, currently proposed at:

- `$7 / month`

### C. Premium Package

One-time package around `$350`

---

## 3. The Premium Package

The `$350` package should no longer be framed as the basic offer.

It becomes the premium, guided, high-touch package.

### Proposed premium package features

- the will form
- full Islamic review
- administrative review
- checking that all important fields are completed correctly
- checking confusing legal terms that the user may misunderstand
- checking uploaded documents and making sure they match the form
- notarization / legal finalization support
- registration in the Canada Will Directory
- 12 months of Muslim Vault access included

### Working explanation of the premium legal layer

The premium package is where the full legal completion happens.

Current working meaning:

- a paralegal or legal professional hosts the Zoom completion session
- the user and witnesses complete the process properly
- the execution event is handled in a professional legal workflow
- the will is then registered in the Canada Will Directory

### Strategic purpose of the premium package

- keep a serious professional offer in the business
- serve users who want full review and completion support
- create a strong upsell from the subscription model
- protect the brand from looking cheap or low-trust

---

## 4. The New Subscription Product

### Product name

`Muslim Vault`

### Core concept

A secure private vault where the user logs in with a username and password and stores sensitive guidance, instructions, notes, and personal messages that should only become available at the right moment.

This is not only a document product.

It is a:

- planning product
- continuity product
- family-clarity product
- access-control product

### Working starter subscription features

The current direction is that the starter plan includes:

- the will form
- full Islamic review
- administrative review
- checking that all important fields are completed correctly
- checking confusing legal terms that the user may misunderstand
- access to the vault
- voice notes
- unlimited updates
- scheduled reminders to review and update the vault

### Why this may still work at `$7 / month`

The business assumption is that much of the starter process can be handled through:

- automation
- structured workflows
- Muslim Will staff support with relatively low delivery cost

This means the starter plan may be viable if the work stays operationally light and standardized.

### Main promise

The user can safely record:

- what people need to know
- what should happen after death
- what should happen during incapacity
- who should receive private messages
- what should remain hidden until the correct moment

---

## 5. Vault Structure

The current concept has two main branches:

### A. After Death Vault

This vault becomes relevant after the user passes away.

### B. POA Vault

This vault becomes relevant if the user is alive but unable to act or communicate.

Note for later discussion:

`POA Vault` may be renamed to something more user-friendly, such as:

- `Incapacity Vault`
- `Emergency Vault`
- `If I Cannot Speak Vault`

For now, we keep the original name in this working idea document.

---

## 6. After Death Vault

### Proposed branches

- My debts
- Money I need to collect from people
- My assets
- My will
- Messages

### Details

#### My debts

The user can record:

- who they owe
- how much
- what type of debt it is
- whether there is supporting evidence
- how it should be settled

#### Money I need to collect from people

The user can record:

- who owes them money
- how much
- why
- whether there is proof
- how the executor should follow up

#### My assets

The user can record asset-location clarity, for example:

- bank information
- safety deposit boxes
- office drawers
- physical keys
- business records
- hidden documents
- account references
- insurance references

Example:

- "I have a vault in HSBC bank and the key is in the drawer of my office."

The purpose is to make hidden or unclear assets easier to discover and manage.

#### My will

The user can keep the will record inside the vault so the executor knows:

- where the will is
- which version is current
- whether supporting documents exist
- which appendices are intended to be part of the full planning record

### Appendix concept inside the will workflow

One important working idea is that some vault records may also be referenced formally in the will package.

Examples:

- Appendix A: list of debts
- Appendix B: money to be collected
- Appendix C: asset reference list

This direction needs careful legal drafting, but the current intention is not to keep the vault completely separate from the will story.

Instead, some parts of the vault may support or connect to the formal will package where appropriate.

#### Messages

This is one of the strongest parts of the idea.

The user can leave:

- written letters
- digital text notes
- voice notes

These can be addressed to:

- a specific person
- a group of people

Examples:

- a message to a father
- a message to a wife
- a message to children
- a message to a business partner

These messages should only be delivered after death, and access behavior should be tracked.

## 7. POA Vault

This vault becomes relevant if the user is alive but incapacitated.

The user stores private instructions for the person who may need to act on their behalf.

### Example use cases

- if I am in a coma
- if I cannot communicate
- if I need medical decisions made
- if my family needs to move temporarily
- if funds are needed urgently
- if relatives must be contacted from abroad

### Example guidance the user may store

- "If I am in this situation, I need my family to move to my original country."
- "I want my friend Hassan to take the medical decisions in my case."
- "If money is needed, contact X or Y."
- "Ask my parents to come from abroad."
- "My son should stay at his college."
- "I want treatment in hospital X."
- "Do not resuscitate me if this specific situation happens."

### Sensitive emotional use case

One important part of the concept is that some instructions are too sensitive to discuss while the user is healthy.

For example:

- the user may trust someone other than the spouse to make medical decisions
- the user may fear emotional conflict if those wishes are discussed too early
- the user may want private direction to be known only when the situation actually happens

This makes the vault different from a simple note-taking app.

It is about:

- privacy
- timing
- controlled disclosure

### POA Vault content types

- written instructions
- structured emergency notes
- voice notes
- private messages

### Medical-wishes handling options

This area should be handled in the most professional way possible.

Current options to consider:

#### Option 1: Guidance only

The vault stores personal wishes and practical instructions, but these are clearly labeled as guidance and not formal medical directives.

#### Option 2: Structured medical-preference section

The user fills a more formal section for:

- preferred hospital
- who should be consulted
- who should make decisions
- travel/contact instructions for relatives
- religious wishes around treatment and end-of-life care

This still needs clear legal wording about what is guidance versus what is legally operative.

#### Option 3: Premium legal medical-direction workflow

The premium package includes a more formal legal/regulated handling of medical wishes through the proper legal document set and professional review.

### Current recommended direction

The strongest working direction is:

- starter plan stores practical medical and incapacity guidance
- premium plan handles the stronger legal side
- the user must be warned that private vault instructions should not conflict with formal legal documents

## 8. The Main Emotional Insight

The idea is built on a very human reality:

- people want to leave clarity
- people want to leave instructions
- people want their loved ones to know what matters
- people often cannot say everything while healthy
- paper notes may be lost
- ordinary digital notes may never reach the right person
- timing matters as much as storage

This means Muslim Vault is not just a storage product.

It is a:

- trust product
- timing product
- dignity product
- legacy product

## 9. Example User Scenario

### Scenario 1: Incapacity

Ahmed joins Muslim Vault.

He chooses the starter package and creates:

- a will
- Islamic review
- administrative review
- Death Vault content
- POA Vault content

He tells his executors or trusted people that he is a Muslim Will member.

Later, Ahmed is in a coma.

The executor or authorized person brings a document proving Ahmed's condition.

Muslim Will verifies the case and gives access to the POA Vault.

Now the relevant person can see Ahmed's instructions, such as:

- where he wants to be treated
- who should be contacted
- who should make medical decisions
- whether money should be requested from specific people
- whether relatives should travel from abroad
- what should happen with children or family arrangements

### Scenario 2: Death

Ahmed passes away.

The executor contacts Muslim Will.

After verification, Muslim Will gives access to:

- Ahmed's will information
- Ahmed's Death Vault

The executor can then:

- identify debts
- identify money owed to Ahmed
- locate assets
- find key practical information
- deliver messages to the right people

### Message-access behavior

Example:

Ahmed leaves a voice note for his father.

The system should instruct the executor not to open it unless required.

The intended behavior is:

- the message is delivered to the father
- if the executor opens it first, the system flags that it was opened
- the system stores a timestamp
- access actions are visible in an audit history

This feature is important because trust depends on accountability.

- includes notarization / legal execution support through the professional Zoom process

### D. Family Package

This should be considered as a dedicated offer for:

- husband
- wife
- adult children

- each person keeps a separate private account
- each vault remains private and secure
- the family receives pricing advantage without merging private data
- family accounts may be linked commercially, but not exposed to one another by default

This can become a very strong offer for Muslim families who want structured planning across the household.

## 13. Trust and Access Logic

This product will depend heavily on trust.

The business must define clearly:

- who can request access
- what proof is needed
- what is unlocked after death
- what is unlocked during incapacity
- who gets notified
- what is logged
- what is visible to executors
- what is visible to POA holders
- what stays private for the final intended recipient only

### Access examples

#### For POA Vault access

Possible proof:

- medical proof
- incapacity confirmation
- POA documentation
- identity verification

#### For After Death Vault access

Possible proof:

- death certificate
- executor verification
- identity verification
- will/executor supporting records

### Identity verification recommendation

The most professional direction is a multi-layered access model:

- username and password
- email verification
- phone verification
- two-factor authentication
- identity confirmation at sensitive release moments
- manual support review for special cases

For high-risk release events, the business should consider a stronger identity process than normal login alone.

### Release-security recommendation

The vault should be sold as a high-security product.

So the working direction should include:

- strong encryption at rest and in transit
- role-based access controls
- secure audit logs
- timestamped access history
- controlled release workflow
- manual verification for death and incapacity release events

### Password storage direction

Current business direction:

- users may store passwords and highly sensitive access information
- this should be treated as a serious security feature, not a casual notes feature

This increases the value of the product, but it also increases the security responsibility of the platform significantly.

## 14. Product Principles

The product should be built around these principles:

### Privacy

No one should see the vault content before the correct trigger.

### Timing

Information should be released only at the right moment.

### Role-based access

Different people should see different things.

### Audit trail

Every opening, access event, and delivery event should be timestamped.

### Clarity

The system should reduce chaos for families.

### Dignity

The product should feel respectful, serious, and private.
