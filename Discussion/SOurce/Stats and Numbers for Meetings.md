# Stats And Numbers For Meetings

Compiled: 12 Apr 2026

Purpose: Give the team a clean, source-backed reference for charity meetings, partner conversations, presentations, follow-up emails, and Q&A. Use these numbers to support the story, not to overload the conversation.

## How To Use This File

- Use the "core stats" in formal presentations and booklet conversations.
- Use the "supporting stats" when answering questions or tailoring the pitch to a specific charity.
- Always label whether a statistic is Canada-specific, U.S.-specific, or broader Islamic philanthropy context.
- Avoid giving legal, tax, or religious advice. Use statistics to support the need for planning and partnership.
- For charity-specific meetings, add the charity's own annual report, donor count, campaign data, or strategic plan wherever possible.

## Core Meeting Narrative

1. Many families do not have an up-to-date will or estate plan.
2. Canada is entering a major wealth-transfer period.
3. Charitable giving is already strong, especially among older donors.
4. Legacy gifts in wills are growing, but still underused.
5. Muslim communities in Canada are large, growing, young, and family-oriented.
6. Muslim Will gives charities a practical way to help supporters protect their families and document charitable intentions.

## Approved Core Stats

### 1. Half Of Canadian Adults Do Not Have A Will

**50%** of Canadian adults do not have a last will and testament. A further **13%** have a will that is out of date.

Use this to say: "The planning gap is not small. Many families are either unprepared or relying on outdated documents."

Source: Angus Reid Institute, "Lacking the Will: Half of Canadians say they don't have a last will and testament", March 2023  
https://angusreid.org/canada-will-testament-intestate-dying-without-will/

### 2. More Than Half Of Canadians Do Not Have An Estate Plan

**54%** of Canadians do not have an estate plan.

Use this to say: "The issue is bigger than a single document. Families often need a clearer planning pathway."

Source: IG Wealth Management / Pollara Strategic Insights, "Estate Planning: A Gift To Your Loved Ones", 2024  
https://www.ig.ca/en/insights/estate-planning

### 3. Canada Is Entering A Major Wealth-Transfer Period

Canada is approaching an estimated **$1 trillion** intergenerational wealth transfer.

Use this to say: "This is the right time for charities to build responsible legacy-giving conversations."

Source: IG Wealth Management, "Estate Planning: A Gift To Your Loved Ones", 2024  
https://www.ig.ca/en/insights/estate-planning

### 4. Canadians Already Give Billions To Charity

Canadian tax filers claimed **$12.8 billion** in charitable donations in 2023. Donors aged 65+ contributed **50.4%** of total donation dollars.

Use this to say: "Charitable giving is already part of Canadian life. Legacy giving helps make future charitable intentions more visible and documented."

Source: Statistics Canada, "Charitable donors, 2023", April 2025  
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/250422/dq250422c-eng.htm

### 5. Gifts In Wills Are Growing, But Still Underused

CAGP / Will Power says charitable bequest participation has moved from **5% to 8%** of Canadians, with a goal of reaching **13% by 2030** and generating an estimated **$58 billion** for social good.

Use this to say: "Legacy giving is already a recognized sector opportunity. The Muslim community needs a trusted, faith-conscious pathway into that conversation."

Source: CAGP / Will Power  
https://www.cagp-acpdp.org/en/will-power

### 6. Even 1% Of An Estate Can Create Impact

Will Power notes that even **1%** of an estate can create a meaningful charitable gift while leaving **99%** for loved ones.

Use this to say: "Legacy giving does not need to compete with family responsibility. It can be family-first and charity-inclusive."

Source: Will Power, "Where to Start"  
https://www.willpower.ca/where-to-start/

### 7. Canada's Muslim Community Is Large And Growing

Statistics Canada's latest Muslim population profile reports **nearly 1.8 million Muslims in Canada**, based on **1,775,715** people identifying as Muslim in the 2021 Census. This represented **4.9%** of Canada's population, up from **2.0%** in 2001.

Use this to say: "This is a large and growing community with real family-planning and charitable-giving needs."

Source: Statistics Canada, "A snapshot of the Muslim population in Canada", based on 2021 Census data  
https://www.statcan.gc.ca/o1/en/plus/7639-snapshot-muslim-population-canada

### 8. Muslim Families Are Younger And Family-Oriented

The median age of Muslims in Canada was **30** in 2021, compared with **41.2** for the total Canadian population. **26.3%** of Muslims in Canada were children aged 0-14.

Use this to say: "Will planning is not only for older donors. For Muslim families, it is also about guardianship, dependents, and family clarity."

Source: Statistics Canada, "A snapshot of the Muslim population in Canada", based on 2021 Census data  
https://www.statcan.gc.ca/o1/en/plus/7639-snapshot-muslim-population-canada

## Useful Phrases For Meetings

**The intention is there. The pathway is often unclear.**

Use when explaining why Muslim Will exists.

**Legacy giving does not need to compete with family responsibility.**

Use when discussing the 1% / 99% point.

**The charity leads with trust. Muslim Will supports the next step.**

Use when clarifying partnership roles.

**The charity remains a trusted guide. Muslim Will provides the planning pathway.**

Use when addressing operational or legal concerns.

**Supporters already care about your mission. The partnership helps them document that care as part of their legacy.**

Use when connecting donor loyalty with gifts in wills.

## Cautions For The Team

- Do not say the charity is giving legal advice.
- Do not say Muslim Will guarantees legal or religious outcomes in every case.
- Do not say every donor should leave a charitable gift.
- Do not pressure families to choose charity over loved ones.
- Say "up to one-third" or "within the permissible bequest portion" when discussing Islamic charitable bequests.
- Use "illustrative example" for estate calculations.
- For Canadian Muslim giving totals, avoid unsupported national estimates. Use Statistics Canada Muslim demographics plus Canadian charitable giving data.
- For U.S. Muslim giving, label the data clearly as U.S.-specific.

## Charity-Specific Prep Checklist

Before a meeting, collect:

- annual report
- audited financial statements
- donor/member count if public or provided
- Ramadan campaign results
- monthly donor program details
- existing planned-giving or legacy-giving page
- major donor language
- strategic priorities
- programs that could be positioned as long-term legacy causes

Use partner-specific data to make the conversation feel relevant to that charity rather than generic.
