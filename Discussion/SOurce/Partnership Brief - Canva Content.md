Charity Partnership Brief

### Subtitle

A practical pathway for Muslim charities helping supporters protect their loved ones and document charitable intentions.

### Opening Copy

Many Muslim supporters already give generously during their lifetime. They give during Ramadan, support campaigns, respond to emergencies, and care deeply about the causes they trust.

But charitable intentions are often not documented in a will. For many families, preparing a will is not rejected. It is delayed because the process can feel complex, sensitive, or difficult to navigate across both legal and Islamic requirements.

Muslim Will helps create a clearer pathway: one that supports family protection, faith-conscious planning, and meaningful charitable legacy giving.

## Why This Matters Now

The intention is there. The pathway is often unclear.

When a will is missing or out of date, important decisions around inheritance, guardianship, final wishes, and charitable giving can be left uncertain. A charity partnership helps supporters begin that planning conversation earlier, with more confidence and less friction.

## Where Charity Partners Fit

Charities can help turn awareness into action.

Supporters often trust the charities they already give to. That trust creates a natural opening for education around will planning, family protection, and charitable legacy giving.

The charity does not need to deliver the will-planning process. Its role is to create awareness, build confidence, and connect supporters to a clearer pathway.

## Three Partner Roles

### Community Awareness

Help supporters understand why will planning matters before it becomes urgent.

### Legacy Education

Encourage earlier thinking around charitable gifts in wills and long-term impact.

### Trusted Connection

Connect interested supporters to a professional will-planning pathway designed for Muslim families.

## Page 2

## What Muslim Will Provides

A guided will-planning pathway for Muslim families.

Muslim Will helps families prepare wills that consider both local legal requirements and Islamic inheritance principles. Through a guided process, families can document key information, clarify important decisions, and receive support around inheritance structure, guardianship, final wishes, and charitable intentions.

## Service Pillars

### Legal Structure

Prepared with attention to Canadian legal requirements and proper execution steps.

### Islamic Inheritance Guidance

Designed to reflect Islamic inheritance principles, including the permissible bequest portion.

### Family Clarity

Helps families document guardianship preferences, distribution intentions, and final wishes.

### Charitable Inclusion

Supports those who want to include charitable gifts while keeping loved ones at the center of their estate plan.

## How The Partnership Works

Clear roles make legacy planning easier to share.

### Muslim Will

Provides the guided will-planning process, educational materials, and support pathway.

### Charity Partner

Builds awareness, trust, and community engagement through existing channels.

### Supporter Journey

Moves from intention to documented will planning.

## Simple Flow

Awareness -> Education -> Guided Planning -> Documented Intentions
