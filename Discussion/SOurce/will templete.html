Administrative Survivorship (Limited Application)

Except where otherwise required by applicable law or expressly stated in this Will, a beneficiary of any discretionary gift, bequest, or portion of my estate not forming part of the mandatory Islamic inheritance (including any Wasiyyah portion not exceeding one-third (1/3) of my estate) must survive me by a period of thirty (30) days to be entitled to receive such benefit.
