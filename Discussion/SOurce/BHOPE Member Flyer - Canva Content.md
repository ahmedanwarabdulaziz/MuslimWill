## Opening Copy

A will is not only about what happens after we leave this world. It is also about responsibility, clarity, and mercy for the people we love.

Through this partnership, BHOPE members and supporters can access a guided will-planning pathway designed for Muslim families in Canada.

## Why This Matters

The intention is there. The pathway is often unclear.

For many Muslim families, preparing a will is not rejected. It is delayed.

Many families delay preparing a will because the process can feel sensitive, confusing, or difficult to navigate across both legal and Islamic requirements.

A clear will can help families document important decisions around inheritance, guardianship, final wishes, and charitable giving.

## What You Can Plan

Protect your loved ones

Without a clear will, loved ones may be left with uncertainty, delays, and difficult decisions during an already painful time.

Clarify guardianship for minor children

If parents do not document guardian preferences, the family may lose control over who is asked to care for the children if both parents pass away.

Document your final wishes

Without written instructions, family members may disagree or feel unsure about your wishes, responsibilities, and final arrangements.

Plan charitable giving within the permissible bequest portion

Many people intend to give sadaqah jariyah, but the intention may never be documented clearly in their will.

Create a clearer path for sadaqah jariyah

A clear will helps turn charitable intention into a planned legacy while keeping family protection at the center.
