# Muslim Will - 10 Facebook-First Social Media Posts

This version replaces carousel/slide posts with Facebook-friendly single-image posts. Each post uses one clear visual, a short text overlay, and a caption that carries the full message.

Source basis: content reviewed from `SOurce` and `SOurce/Email Journey`, including the business summary, charity booklet, partnership brief, decision sheet, BHOPE flyer, stats reference, FAQ questions, email journey documents, PDF journey report, and Ontario will template.

## Facebook Content Direction

- Use one strong image per post, not multiple slides.
- Keep on-image text very short. Aim for one scroll-stopping hook, not an explanation.
- Let the caption explain the idea in a warm, human way.
- Use real family, community, charity, or document-planning visuals where possible.
- Avoid fear-based images, funeral imagery, or anything that feels heavy.
- Keep the design calm, premium, and easy to read on mobile.
- Use brand colors: deep green `#004926`, warm cream `#eeeedd`, gold accent `#d4af37`.
- Image prompts should generate realistic photo-style visuals only. Add all text, logos, QR codes, brand frames, and source notes later in Canva.
- Use the footer line where appropriate: "Professional will planning, guided by Islamic principles."
- Avoid risky claims such as "guaranteed", "100% Shariah-compliant", or "government approved".

## Facebook Design Checklist

- Use one image only per post.
- Keep overlay text as a short hook, ideally one sentence or phrase.
- Separate the hook from functional details such as partner name, QR code, or pricing.
- Generate background images only: no text, no logos, no QR codes, no watermarks.
- Ask for realistic photography with mostly Middle Eastern/Arab Muslim people, and occasional South Asian Muslim diversity where relevant.
- Avoid asking Gemini to create frames, borders, colored panels, or designed backgrounds. Add brand colors and frames in Canva.
- Use the caption for explanation, not the image.
- Use warm family and community images more often than abstract graphics.
- Use stats sparingly, and make the number the visual focus.
- Put QR codes only on partner/access posts, not every awareness post.
- Keep gold as an accent, not the main color.
- Keep the family-first message visible whenever charity is mentioned.
- Add source notes only in small text for stat graphics.
