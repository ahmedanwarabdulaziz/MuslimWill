## Opening Line

Supporters already care about your mission. A partnership helps them document that care as part of their legacy.

## The Opportunity

Many Muslim families want to protect their loved ones, follow Islamic inheritance principles, and give meaningfully. But will planning is often delayed because the pathway is unclear.

For charities, this creates a practical opportunity: help supporters begin the conversation, while Muslim Will provides the guided will-planning process.

## What The Partnership Does

Creates awareness around will planning.

Educates supporters about family protection and charitable legacy giving.

Connects interested supporters to a guided Muslim will-planning pathway.

Helps charities build a structured legacy-giving conversation without taking on legal or operational responsibility.
