## Page 2: Why This Matters Now

### Main Headline

**For many Muslim families, preparing a will is not rejected. It is delayed.**

### Body Copy

The reason is rarely a lack of concern. More often, the process feels complex, sensitive, or difficult to navigate across both legal and Islamic requirements.

When a will is missing or out of date, important decisions around family protection, inheritance, guardianship, and charitable giving can be left uncertain.

### Stat Callouts

**50%**  
of Canadian adults do not have a will.

**13%**  
have a will that is out of date.

**54%**  
of Canadians do not have an estate plan.

### Closing Line

**A clearer planning pathway helps families move from intention to action.**

### Source Notes

- Angus Reid Institute, "Lacking the Will: Half of Canadians say they don't have a last will and testament", March 2023  
  https://angusreid.org/canada-will-testament-intestate-dying-without-will/
- IG Wealth Management / Pollara Strategic Insights, "Estate Planning: A Gift To Your Loved Ones", 2024  
  https://www.ig.ca/en/insights/estate-planning

### Design Notes

- Use a clean, executive layout.
- Recommended layout: headline and body copy on the left, three stat callouts on the right or across the bottom.
- Keep the stat numbers large and easy to scan.
- Use deep green for the headline and numbers.
- Use gold only as a small accent, divider, or number highlight.
- Avoid fear-based imagery.
- Optional visual: subtle document/checklist icon or simple will-planning line illustration.
- Keep source notes very small at the bottom if included in the designed booklet.
- In the designed footer, use "IG Wealth Management", not "IC Wealth Management".

### Page Purpose

This page should establish the planning gap and create urgency without sounding alarming. The charity should understand that will planning is a real community need, not just a product offer.

**26.3%**

**When families are young, growing, and building for the future, planning should not wait for a crisis.**

  https://www.statcan.gc.ca/o1/en/plus/7639-snapshot-muslim-population-canada

- Recommended layout: headline and body copy on the left, four compact stat callouts on the right or in a 2x2 grid.
- Keep the page calm and family-centered.
- Use deep green for key numbers and headings.
- Use gold sparingly for a divider, small label, or highlight.
- Avoid overloading the page with demographic detail.
- Optional visual: subtle family/community icon or a soft geometric accent.

## Page 4: The Legacy Giving Opportunity

### Main Headline

**Supporters already give. Legacy planning helps them give with lasting intention.**

### Body Copy

Many charity supporters give generously during their lifetime through campaigns, monthly donations, events, and seasonal appeals.

But charitable intentions are often not documented in a will. When that happens, long-term giving can remain informal, unclear, or never completed.

For charities, this creates an opportunity to help supporters think earlier and more clearly about the causes they want to continue supporting.

### Stat Callouts

**$12.8B**  
in charitable donations claimed by Canadian tax filers in 2023.

**50.4%**  
of total donation dollars came from donors aged 65+ in 2023.

**5% to 8%**  
charitable bequest participation growth reported by CAGP / Will Power.

**13% by 2030**  
CAGP / Will Power's participation goal, with an estimated $58B for social good.

### Closing Line

**Legacy giving grows when donor intention becomes part of a clear planning process.**

### Source Notes

- Statistics Canada, "Charitable donors, 2023", April 2025  
  https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/250422/dq250422c-eng.htm
- CAGP / Will Power, national charitable gifts in wills campaign  
  https://www.cagp-acpdp.org/en/will-power

### Design Notes

- This page should feel opportunity-focused, not fundraising-heavy.
- Recommended layout: one short narrative section plus three or four strong stat callouts.
- Use a warmer visual rhythm than Page 2 and Page 3 so the booklet starts to shift toward opportunity.
- Consider using a small "lifetime giving to legacy giving" visual flow.
- Keep the phrase "legacy giving" prominent.
- Avoid making the page feel like an ROI forecast.
- Keep source notes small at the bottom.

### Page Purpose

This page should help charity leaders see why will planning matters to them: it connects existing donor generosity with documented long-term charitable impact.

## Page 5: Where Charity Partners Fit

### Main Headline

**Charities can help turn awareness into action.**

### Body Copy

Supporters often trust the charities they already give to. That trust creates a natural opening for education around will planning, family protection, and charitable legacy giving.

The charity does not need to deliver the will-planning process. Its role is to create awareness, build confidence, and connect supporters to a clearer pathway.

### Three Partner Roles

**Community Awareness**  
Help supporters understand why will planning matters before it becomes urgent.

**Legacy Education**  
Encourage earlier thinking around charitable gifts in wills and long-term impact.

**Trusted Connection**  
Connect interested supporters to a professional will-planning pathway designed for Muslim families.

### Closing Line

**A strong partnership helps turn donor intention into documented legacy planning.**

### Design Notes

- Recommended layout: three equal cards or columns.
- Use one simple icon per role:
  - Community Awareness: lightbulb, announcement, or community icon.
  - Legacy Education: open book or learning icon.
  - Trusted Connection: handshake, link, or pathway icon.
- Keep the body copy short and leadership-friendly.
- Avoid implying that the charity provides legal advice or religious rulings.
- No stats are required on this page.
- This page can use more white space and icons to create a break from the previous data-heavy pages.

### Page Purpose

This page should clarify the charity's role in the partnership. The charity should feel that its role is meaningful, trusted, and low-burden.

## Page 6: What Muslim Will Provides

### Main Headline

**A guided will-planning pathway for Muslim families.**

### Body Copy

Muslim Will helps families prepare wills that consider both local legal requirements and Islamic inheritance principles.

Through a guided process, families can document key information, clarify important decisions, and receive support around inheritance structure, guardianship, final wishes, and charitable intentions.

### Service Pillars

**Legal Structure**  
Prepared with attention to Canadian legal requirements and execution steps.

**Islamic Inheritance Guidance**  
Designed to support Islamic inheritance principles, including madhhab-aware considerations where applicable.

**Family Clarity**  
Helps families document guardianship preferences, distribution intentions, and final wishes.

**Charitable Inclusion**  
Supports supporters who want to include charitable gifts within the permissible bequest portion.

### Closing Line

**A clearer process makes responsible, faith-conscious planning easier to begin.**

### Design Notes

- Recommended layout: four service pillars in a 2x2 grid or four horizontal cards.
- Use one simple icon per pillar:
  - Legal Structure: document or shield icon.
  - Islamic Inheritance Guidance: book or guidance icon.
  - Family Clarity: family or home icon.
  - Charitable Inclusion: heart/hand or giving icon.
- Keep the page explanatory, not overly detailed.
- Avoid language that sounds like a legal guarantee.
- Keep the tone professional, supportive, and trust-building.

### Page Purpose

This page should explain the actual service in a simple way. Charity leaders should understand what their supporters receive and why Muslim Will is a relevant partner.
