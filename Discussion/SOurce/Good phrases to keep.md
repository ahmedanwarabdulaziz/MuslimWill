# Good Phrases To Keep

## Booklet And Partnership Language

**The intention is there. The pathway is often unclear.**

Use for a transition page, solution page, or partnership explanation. This phrase works well when explaining why Muslim Will exists: families and donors often care deeply, but they need a clearer way to act.

**For many Muslim families, preparing a will is not rejected. It is delayed.**

Use when speaking to members or supporters. This avoids blame and makes the problem feel practical: people care, but the process feels sensitive, complex, or unclear.

**Legacy giving does not need to compete with family responsibility.**

Use when explaining charitable bequests in a calm, family-first way. This works well beside the 1% / 99% message or when speaking to charity partners.

**The charity leads with trust. Muslim Will supports the next step.**

Use when clarifying that the charity is not giving legal or religious advice. The charity creates awareness and connection; Muslim Will provides the will-planning pathway.

**The charity remains a trusted guide. Muslim Will provides the planning pathway.**

Use in partnership decks, booklets, and meeting follow-ups when a charity may be worried about operational or legal responsibility.

**Supporters already care about your mission. The partnership helps them document that care as part of their legacy.**

Use when connecting donor loyalty with charitable gifts in wills.

## Arabic And English Brand Language

### Arabic

**الوصية... أمانةٌ ورحمة**

**أمانةٌ نؤديها لله، ورحمةٌ نتركها لأهلنا من بعدنا.**

فهي ليست حديثًا عن الرحيل،
بقدر ما هي حديثٌ عن مسؤوليةٍ لا ينبغي أن تُؤجَّل،
وعن عبدٍ يحرص أن يلقى الله وقد أدّى ما عليه،
وأن يترك من بعده أمرًا واضحًا، وحقًّا محفوظًا، ورحمةً تمتدّ إلى أهله،
حتى لا يترك بعده حيرةً، ولا نزاعًا، ولا حقوقًا معلّقة،
بل يترك وضوحًا، وعدلًا، ورحمة.

Short version:

**الوصية... أمانةٌ ورحمة**

**أمانةٌ نؤديها لله، ورحمةٌ نتركها لأهلنا من بعدنا.**

### English

**A will is a trust and a mercy.**

**A trust we fulfill before Allah, and a mercy we leave for our families after us.**

It is not only a conversation about departure.
It is a conversation about a responsibility that should not be delayed,
about a servant who hopes to meet Allah having fulfilled what was due,
and about leaving behind clarity, protected rights, and mercy for the family.

So that what remains is not confusion, dispute, or unresolved rights,
but clarity, justice, and mercy.

Short version:

**A will is a trust and a mercy.**

**A trust we fulfill before Allah, and a mercy we leave for our families after us.**

Use this for emotionally resonant Arabic/English content: homepage sections, Ramadan campaigns, mosque/community presentations, booklet openings, or video scripts. It is strongest when used as a values statement, not as legal or technical copy.

## Website Legal And Trust Language

**Create a lawyer-prepared Islamic will designed to be legally valid under Ontario law when properly signed and witnessed.**

Use as a strong homepage or pricing-page promise when the service is Ontario-specific and the will is prepared by licensed legal professionals. This keeps the legal claim clear without saying the will is "government approved."

**Recognized under applicable provincial law when properly executed.**

Use in compliance copy, FAQ answers, and signing guidance. This is a safer phrase than "approved by the Canadian government" because wills are generally recognized when they meet provincial legal requirements.

**Prepared with attention to Ontario legal requirements and Islamic inheritance principles.**

Use when we want a slightly softer sentence than "legally valid." This works well in explanatory sections.

**Designed to reflect Islamic inheritance principles within the Canadian legal context.**

Use when discussing Shariah-conscious planning without implying that every unique case receives a binding fatwa.

**Legal validity depends on accurate information, proper review, and correct signing and witnessing.**

Use in FAQ, compliance, and checkout flows to set the right expectations before users sign.

**Muslim Will helps you move from intention to a properly prepared and executed will.**

Use as a bridge between emotional copy and process copy.

## Phrases To Avoid Or Replace

**Avoid: Approved by the Canadian government.**

Replace with: **Recognized under applicable provincial law when properly executed.**

**Avoid: 100% Shariah-compliant.**

Replace with: **Designed to reflect Islamic inheritance principles.**

**Avoid: Guaranteed legal validity in every case.**

Replace with: **Designed to be legally valid under Ontario law when properly signed and witnessed.**

**Avoid: The charity helps donors write legal wills.**

Replace with: **The charity creates awareness and connects supporters to a professional will-planning pathway.**
