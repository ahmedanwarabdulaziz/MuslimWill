# Muslim Will - Suggested Process Shifts And Ideas

Purpose: capture improvement ideas to consider after the current manual email journey is stable.

## Suggested Process Shifts

| Idea | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Future payment placement | Consider testing payment after intake or after a short pre-intake step. | This may reduce payment friction, but it should be tested carefully because unpaid intake submissions can create operational load. |
| Secure ID upload | Move from email-based ID submission to a secure upload link when possible. | IDs are sensitive. A controlled upload flow is stronger than email or WhatsApp. |
| Section-specific help | Use the stopped intake section to send targeted support and educational links. | A customer stuck on guardianship needs different help than a customer stuck on charitable giving. |
| Meeting 1 as quality control | Keep Meeting 1 thorough and use it to confirm witness and ID readiness. | This protects Meeting 2 so it can remain a short 10-minute professional finalization session. |
| Manual operations tracker | Maintain one tracker row per client until automation exists. | The tracker prevents missed follow-ups, duplicate messages, and unclear ownership. |
| Soft post-completion follow-up | Keep review/referral requests optional and values-based. | The client has completed a serious family document. The follow-up should feel grateful, not promotional. |

## Ideas To Revisit Later

- Add secure file upload for IDs and sensitive documents.
- Add system-generated staff tasks for follow-up dates.
- Add clear status labels for account, payment, intake, review, attestation, and final delivery.
- Add section-specific help links inside intake reminders.
- Consider future Arabic and French journeys after the English journey is stable.
- Consider a separate BHOPE journey with partner-specific language and timing.
