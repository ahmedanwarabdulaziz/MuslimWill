# Muslim Will Customer Email Journey - Structure

Working draft for discussion and refinement.

Note: The A4 HTML report is now the master working document for the current plan. This Markdown file is an earlier structure draft and may not include the latest confirmed operating decisions.

## Purpose

This document organizes the customer email journey for Muslim Will from pre-account interest through completion and long-term review.

The goal is to make every customer feel clearly guided, professionally supported, and never unsure about the next step.

## Brand Tone

Use a calm, professional, human tone.

Recommended brand footer line:

**Professional will planning, guided by Islamic principles.**

Avoid language that feels overly sales-driven, overly legal-heavy, or fear-based.

## Core Communication Principles

| Principle | Instruction |
|---|---|
| One clear purpose per email | Each email should focus on one customer action or one progress update. |
| Make the next step obvious | Every action-based email should include one primary CTA. |
| Reduce anxiety | Explain where the customer is in the process and what happens next. |
| Separate action emails from status emails | Action emails ask the customer to do something. Status emails reassure them that progress is happening. |
| Use reminders carefully | Reminder sequences should be stronger when the customer has not created an account, paid, completed intake, or booked a required session. |
| Keep legal/religious boundaries clear | Do not imply guaranteed legal validity, binding religious rulings, or that Muslim Will is a law firm or religious authority. |

## Reminder Strategy Overview

| Phase | Reminder Need | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting List / Before Account Creation | High | Use a strong but respectful reminder sequence. The customer has not entered the system yet. |
| Account Created / Payment Not Completed | High | Remind quickly. Customer showed intent but did not finish payment. |
| Payment Completed / Intake Not Completed | High | Tone should shift from marketing to client support. They are now a customer. |
| Intake In Progress | Medium to High | Remind if the intake is inactive. Offer support and educational help. |
| Intake Submitted | Low | No reminder needed unless there is a delay before the next stage. |
| Meeting 1 Booking | High | Remind until the customer books. |
| Meeting 1 Preparation | High | Send scheduled reminders before the meeting, especially around ID submission. |
| After Meeting 1 | Low to Medium | Send progress updates if amendments are needed or if there is a delay. |
| Islamic Review Started | Low | Status update only. Send delay update if review takes longer than expected. |
| Islamic Review Complete | Medium to High | Remind if the customer does not book the attestation session. |
| Meeting 2 Booking | High | Remind until the final session is booked. |
| Meeting 2 Preparation | High | Send scheduled reminders to prepare witnesses, IDs, and video/session requirements. |
| Completion Confirmation | None | No reminder. This is a closing email. |
| Annual Review | Scheduled | Send annually and after major life-event prompts where possible. |

## Detailed Reminder Sequences

### 1. WL - Waiting List / Before Account Creation

Main email:

**Your Muslim Will Access Is Ready**

| Timing | Email Code | Reminder Headline | Purpose | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | WL-01 | **Your Muslim Will Access Is Ready** | Invite the waiting-list customer to create an account. | Create Your Account |
| Day 2 | WL-R1 | **A Quick Reminder to Create Your Muslim Will Account** | Gentle nudge after no action. | Create Your Account |
| Day 5 | WL-R2 | **Your Access Is Still Available** | Remind them they can still begin. | Create Your Account |
| Day 10 | WL-R3 | **Many Families Delay Will Planning. You Can Start Clearly.** | Educational reminder, not just a sales nudge. | Start Clearly |
| Day 14 | WL-R4 | **Final Reminder: Your Muslim Will Access Is Waiting** | Last direct reminder before slower nurture. | Create Your Account |
| Day 30 | WL-R5 | **Still Thinking About Your Will? We Are Here to Help** | Soft long-term re-engagement. | Ask a Question / Create Account |

Instruction:

The first two emails can be direct. Later reminders should become more educational and supportive, not pushy.

### 2A. PAY - Account Created But Payment Not Completed

Main email:

**Complete Your Setup to Continue**

| Timing | Email Code | Reminder Headline | Purpose | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate / Day 0 | PAY-01 | **Complete Your Setup to Continue** | Tell the customer payment is needed to continue. | Complete Payment |
| 1 hour later | PAY-R1 | **Your Muslim Will Setup Is Almost Complete** | Catch customers who got distracted or had payment friction. | Complete Payment |
| 24 hours later | PAY-R2 | **Complete Your Payment to Continue Your Will Package** | Clear action reminder. | Complete Payment |
| 3 days later | PAY-R3 | **Need Help Completing Your Setup?** | Offer support and remove friction. | Get Help / Complete Payment |
| 7 days later | PAY-R4 | **Your Account Is Ready When You Are** | Softer reminder with lower pressure. | Complete Payment |
| 14 days later | PAY-R5 | **A Final Reminder to Continue Your Muslim Will Setup** | Last direct conversion reminder. | Complete Payment |

This sequence is still partly conversion-focused. Keep it professional and helpful. Avoid making the customer feel chased.

### 14. REV - Post-Completion / Annual Review

Main email:

**A Simple Reminder to Review Your Will**

| Timing | Email Code | Headline | Purpose | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 months after completion | REV-01 | **A Simple Reminder to Review Your Will** | Encourage annual review. | Review Your Will |
| Life-event campaign | REV-L1 | **When Life Changes, Your Will May Need to Change Too** | Educate around life events that may require updates. | Request a Review |

Common life events to mention:

- marriage
- divorce
- birth or adoption of a child
- property purchase
- major change in assets
- relocation to another province, state, or country
- death or unavailability of an executor
- death or unavailability of a guardian
- change in charitable intentions

Instruction:

Long-term emails should maintain trust and usefulness, not pressure customers into unnecessary updates.

## Working Assumptions To Confirm

- Customers may join through referrals, webinars, community partners, social media, or waiting list campaigns.
- Customers create an account before completing their full intake.
- Some customers create an account but do not complete payment.
- Some customers pay but do not complete the intake form.
- Meeting 1 is a document review session before Islamic compliance review.
- Islamic review happens after Meeting 1 and any required amendments.
- Meeting 2 is the attestation/finalization session.
- Witnesses and IDs are required before the final stage can proceed.
- Post-completion follow-up should include review, referral, and annual update logic.
