Core Communication Principles

The goal is to make every customer feel clearly guided, professionally supported, and never unsure about the next step.

PrincipleInstruction
One clear purpose per emailEach email should focus on one customer action or one progress update.
Make the next step obviousEvery action-based email should include one primary CTA.
Reduce anxietyExplain where the customer is in the process and what happens next.
Separate action emails from status emailsAction emails ask the customer to do something. Status emails reassure them that progress is happening.
Use reminders carefullyReminder sequences should be stronger when the customer has not created an account, paid, completed intake, or booked a required session.
Keep legal and religious boundaries clearDo not imply guaranteed legal validity, binding religious rulings, or that Muslim Will is a law firm or religious authority.

Compliance Language To Use

These are protective wording blocks for staff use. Final legal wording should be reviewed by the appropriate legal professional.

StageRecommended Compliance Language
General process emailsThis email is provided for administrative and informational purposes related to your Muslim Will document process. It is not legal advice, tax advice, financial advice, or a religious ruling. Legal validity depends on accurate information, appropriate review, and proper signing and witnessing according to the requirements that apply in your jurisdiction.
Islamic review emailsIslamic review is intended to support faith-conscious planning and Islamic inheritance considerations. It does not replace a personal fatwa or independent religious advice for complex circumstances.
Legal / signing process emailsMuslim Will helps coordinate a guided will-planning process. Requirements may vary by province, state, country, family circumstances, and document type. You are responsible for providing complete and accurate information and for following the signing and witnessing instructions provided.
Marketing / waiting list emailsYou are receiving this email because you requested information, joined a waiting list, attended a session, or otherwise expressed interest in Muslim Will. You may unsubscribe from non-transactional messages at any time.
Sensitive document handlingFor sensitive documents, please use the official submission method provided by our team. Do not send personal identification documents through informal channels unless specifically instructed by Muslim Will.
Instruction: Keep promotional wording out of critical service emails where possible. Service emails should focus on the client's document process and required actions.
