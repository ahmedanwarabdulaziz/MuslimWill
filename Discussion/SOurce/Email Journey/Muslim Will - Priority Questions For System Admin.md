# Muslim Will - Priority Questions For System Admin

Purpose: clarify the system and workflow points that affect email wording, manual tracking, and future automation.

## Priority Questions

| # | Admin Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are Power of Attorney documents always included, or only included when the customer selects them? | The Meeting 1 email must not tell every client that all four documents are included if PoA is optional. |
| 2 | What exact system statuses exist for account created, payment page visited, payment completed, payment failed, and intake submitted? | The team needs accurate language and clean manual tracking. Do not use payment-failure wording unless the system confirms it. |
| 3 | Can the system flag paid customers who have not completed intake after 2 days? | This is the agreed point when a paid client should be treated as stuck and contacted personally. |
| 4 | What exact stopped-section labels does the system show, and can they be exported or copied into the tracker? | The system can detect stopped sections; the team needs consistent labels for section-specific reminders. |
| 5 | Can the system require or clearly collect witness information and witness IDs before Meeting 1? | Witnesses and IDs are needed early so Meeting 2 can stay short and professional. |
| 6 | Will ID submission remain by email, or should a secure upload link be added? | IDs are sensitive documents. A secure upload method would be stronger for privacy and process control. |
| 7 | Can the system create internal staff reminders for manual follow-up dates? | Emails are manual for now, so the team needs reliable task prompts. |
| 8 | Can the system track final delivery status: email sent, printed copy prepared, printed copy delivered? | Completion is not only document signing. Delivery must also be traceable. |
| 9 | If payment moves after intake in the future, what system and email status changes will be required? | The current journey assumes payment before intake, but the future flow may change. |

## Notes For Admin Discussion

- BHOPE customers are excluded from this general journey and will need a separate journey.
- Current payment flow is payment before intake.
- Future process may move payment after intake, but this requires a separate system and email review.
- Meeting 1 should prepare the client thoroughly so Meeting 2 can stay short and professional.
- IDs and witness information are operationally critical before Meeting 1 under the current process.
