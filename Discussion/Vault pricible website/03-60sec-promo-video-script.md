**Angle:** Fear/protection open → full 4-branch Vault story → emotional peak on Final Words → calm resolution
**Voiceover:** English, with light Islamic phrasing (amanah, restrained tone)
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok / Facebook feed — assume sound off by default, so every VO line is also burned in as a caption

## 1. Concept in One Line

A man quietly puts his affairs in order while he's alive and well — and months later, we see his family receiving exactly what they need, exactly when they need it. No funeral imagery, no grief theatrics. The emotion comes from ordinary, tender moments: a key, a note, a video message finally watched.

## 3. Shot-by-Shot AI Video Generation Prompts

Most AI video tools (Sora, Runway Gen-4, Kling, Veo 3) cap individual generations at 5–10 seconds, so this 60-second ad is stitched from **10 short clips**. Generate each separately, then edit together in order. Prompts below are written ready to paste in.

**Clip 1 (0:00–0:03) — Hook**
> Extreme close-up, shallow depth of field, a middle-aged man's hands resting on a wooden desk beside a smartphone. The phone screen lights up with an incoming call, warm lamp light, soft shadows, cinematic, 24fps, shot on 50mm lens. Sound: a single phone ring, then cut to black.

**Clip 2 (0:03–0:07) — Chaos 1**
> A modest living room, overcast daylight through a window. A woman in her early 30s sits on the floor, surrounded by opened envelopes and paperwork, phone pressed to her ear, quiet, overwhelmed expression, handheld camera movement, muted natural color grade, documentary style.

**Clip 3 (0:07–0:12) — Chaos 2**
> A home office. A man in his late 20s opens drawers one after another, searching, frustrated but quiet, whispers inaudibly to someone off-frame. Handheld, natural window light, shallow focus on his hands and face.

**Clip 4 (0:12–0:16) — The Turn**
> Same desk as clip 1, same man, alive and relaxed, ordinary evening at home, warm lamp light. He opens a laptop; the screen shows a simple, calm app interface glowing softly — treat the screen content as real, not as a graphic overlay. Gentle push-in camera move.

**Clip 5 (0:16–0:22) — Debts**
> Close-up on a man's hand writing on a small paper notepad in neat handwriting, then his other hand tapping the same words into a smartphone note app. Warm, calm lighting, slow deliberate movement, shallow depth of field.

**Clip 6 (0:22–0:28) — Assets**
> A man's hand holding up a small brass safety-deposit-box key toward the camera, softly lit, then cut to his hand typing a short note into a phone. Calm, precise, warm indoor lighting.

**Clip 7 (0:28–0:34) — Sealed Disclosures**
> Close-up: a man sealing a plain white envelope, writing a single name on the front in pen, then placing it into a wooden drawer that closes with a soft, quiet click. Warm, intimate lighting, shallow focus.

**Clip 8 (0:34–0:41) — Final Words**
> A man props a smartphone against a lamp on a desk, sits back in a chair, takes a breath, and begins speaking directly to the camera with a soft, emotional expression, eyes glistening. Natural warm lighting, static camera framing like a selfie video, intimate and quiet.

**Clip 9 (0:41–0:49) — Emotional Peak**
> A young woman in her mid-20s alone in a softly lit bedroom, sitting on her bed, headphones in, watching a video on her phone. Her expression shifts from stillness to quiet tears, then a small emotional smile. Soft natural light through a curtain, slow gentle push-in.

**Clip 10 (0:49–1:00) — Resolution + End Card**
> Wide shot, golden hour light through a window, a small family — a young woman, a young man, an older woman — seated together at a home dining table, calm and at ease, quiet conversation, warm natural tones. Cut to: clean end card, soft sage-green and cream background, a phone mockup centered showing an app interface, logo text settling in center, calm and minimal, no clutter.

## 4. Casting & Consistency Notes

AI video tools generate each clip independently, so the same "characters" will drift in appearance across clips unless you lock them down:

- **Use one reference image per character** (the father, the daughter, the brother) and feed it into every clip that includes them, using whichever consistency feature your tool supports (character reference / image-to-video seed / face-lock).
- Keep **wardrobe identical** across all of that character's clips — note it explicitly in every prompt if your tool doesn't support image reference (e.g., "same man from clip 1, navy sweater, short grey beard").
- Keep **lighting style consistent** (warm, natural, home-interior) across the whole first act (clips 1–8) so the resolution + end card (clips 9–10) feel like a deliberate shift into calm, not a jarring style change.

---

## 5. Audio Direction

- **VO tone:** calm, low register, unhurried — never dramatic or sales-y. Think a trusted narrator, not an ad announcer.
- **Music:** soft solo piano or oud from 0:00, minimal strings enter around 0:34 (Final Words), swells gently at 0:41 (peak), resolves to a warm, settled chord by 0:55.
- **SFX:** phone ring (0:00), pen on paper (0:16), envelope seal + drawer click (0:28), soft room tone throughout — no stock "epic" swooshes.
- **No spoken religious phrases voiced aloud** in this cut — Islamic framing stays implicit (calm, dignity, restraint) rather than quoted, so the ad reads as universal at first watch and Islamic on reflection. If you want an explicit line, the only candidate for a single spoken Arabic word is *"amanah"* placed right after "It doesn't have to be this way" (0:16) — optional, test both versions.
