# Muslim Will — Vault Model: Strategy & Core Decisions

## Status
Working document. Use this as the source of truth before any website or product work begins.

---

## 1. The Strategic Shift

The business is moving away from a single $350 will purchase toward a dual model built on:

- a low-friction monthly subscription that acquires as many users as possible
- a premium one-time package for users who want full human review, legal attestation, and complete guided service

The concept name for this direction: **The Bridge**

The core idea: bridge the user's intentions to their family, bridge Islamic principles to Canadian law, and bridge their life to what comes after.

---

## 2. Why This Shift

The $350 one-time offer faces direct market resistance because:

- free will websites exist
- some mosques and Islamic centers offer wills for free or by donation
- users compare Muslim Will on price alone before understanding the deeper value
- the decision to spend $350 feels high-risk for a first interaction

The new model solves this by:

- removing the upfront $350 decision entirely
- letting the user enter for $5.99/month
- building trust and emotional investment through the vault
- making the $350 full package feel like a natural and obvious upgrade later

## 4. The Vault — 4 Branches

### Branch 1: My Debts

The user records:
- who they owe money to
- how much
- type of debt
- any supporting documents attached
- instructions for how it should be settled

On death verification, Muslim Will passes this to the executor with the attached documents.

### Branch 2: My Assets

The user records:
- bank accounts
- property
- safety deposit boxes
- business interests
- physical keys or document locations
- insurance references
- any hidden or unclear assets

Purpose: help the family locate and manage everything after death without confusion.

### Branch 3: My Documents

The user uploads any document they consider important:
- ID documents
- property documents
- business records
- insurance policies
- contracts
- anything the family may need

No fixed structure — flexible storage for whatever the user needs to preserve.

### Branch 4: My Messages

The user records:
- written letters
- audio clips
- video clips

Each message is addressed to a specific person or group:
- a message for my brother
- a message for my mother
- a message for a friend
- a message for my business partner

Messages are stored securely and forwarded after death to the named recipients, after Muslim Will receives official death verification.

Access is tracked — the system logs every open event with a timestamp so trust and accountability are maintained.

---

## 5. POA / Incapacity Integration

The vault also covers the scenario where the user is alive but incapacitated (coma, medical emergency, inability to communicate).

The user can store:
- POA documents (who should act on their behalf)
- private instructions for the POA holder
- medical wishes
- emergency contact directions
- financial access instructions
- family arrangements

The POA vault is released when Muslim Will receives official documentation of incapacity, not death.

This means Muslim Will is relevant to two major life scenarios:
1. Death
2. Incapacity

This expands the product beyond a will company into a full life-continuity product.

## 9. Positioning Direction

### The old positioning
"Create your Islamic will."

### The new positioning
"Protect your family with a will and a private legacy vault."

Or more precisely:

> Muslim Will gives you a complete Islamic will and a private secure vault — so your family knows what they need to know, receives what you want them to receive, and is never left without answers.

### What makes this different from free will websites

Free templates may offer a basic document. They do not offer:
- timed private message delivery (audio + video)
- asset and debt clarity for executors
- document storage with executor access
- incapacity instructions and POA support
- Islamic inheritance compliance built in
- verified scholar and legal review (as an add-on)
- digital attestation
- audit-tracked access events
