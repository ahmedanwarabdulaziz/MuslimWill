## Critical Pricing Psychology Rule

The $7.99/month package must feel **complete and premium**, not entry-level or cheap.

The add-ons must be framed as **"for special or complex situations"** — not as upgrades that complete the package.

The message to the customer must be:
> "The Bridge gives you everything most Muslim families need. The add-ons exist for specific legal situations that not everyone requires."

Examples of correct framing:
- ✓ "Digital Attestation — for those who need their will formally witnessed and legally certified"
- ✓ "Scholar Review — for complex inheritance situations or large/unusual estates"
- ✓ "Admin Review — for clients with multiple properties, business assets, or cross-border considerations"

Examples of wrong framing:
- ✗ "Upgrade your will with scholar review"
- ✗ "Make your will complete with attestation"
- ✗ "Add what's missing"

The customer who picks $7.99 should feel they made the smart, complete choice — not that they picked the budget option.

- Debts may go unpaid — a major sin in Islam

The Prophet ﷺ said:
*"It is not permissible for any Muslim who has something to bequeath to sleep two nights without having his last will and testament written and kept ready with him."*
(Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

A will is not just legal protection. It is an Islamic obligation.

## 12. Visual and Tone Direction

### Tone
- Calm but urgent
- Islamic without being old-fashioned
- Premium without being cold
- Personal and direct — speaks to one person, not a demographic

### Visual
- Deep green, cream, gold
- Clean typography, generous spacing
- No clutter
- The vault should feel like something private and precious, not a filing cabinet
- The messages section should feel emotional — soft, warm, human
