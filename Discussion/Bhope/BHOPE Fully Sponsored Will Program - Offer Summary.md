# BHOPE Fully Sponsored Will Program - Offer Summary

Source reviewed: `Bhope/muslimwill-bhope-v2.html`

Purpose of this file: summarize the new BHOPE offer idea clearly for business, marketing, operational, and web-content discussion. This is not final public copy. It is a working strategy summary.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

BHOPE's new idea is to move from a discounted member-access offer to a sponsored will model.

Instead of asking BHOPE members or supporters to pay a reduced fee for Muslim Will, BHOPE would sponsor the cost of selected will packages. The community member receives access at no personal cost, and as part of the sponsored pathway they are invited, or potentially required depending on final policy, to include a charitable wasiyyah/bequest to BHOPE or one of BHOPE's programs.

In simple terms:

**BHOPE sponsors the will today. The supporter documents a future charitable legacy for BHOPE tomorrow.**

This turns the partnership into a fully sponsored legacy-giving program, not just a discount promotion.

---

## 2. Core Offer Idea

The proposed offer is:

**BHOPE will sponsor a limited number of Muslim Will packages for eligible community members, allowing them to create their Islamic will at no personal cost, while including a charitable bequest to support BHOPE's work.**

The current HTML presents the offer as:

- User cost: free
- Sponsor: BHOPE
- Document type: Islamic will
- Charitable bequest: at least 5% of net worth
- Beneficiary: BHOPE
- Optional program choice: medical aid, education, or emergency relief
- Framing: family protection today, sadaqah jariyah tomorrow

This offer is stronger than a discount because the main conversion barrier, price, is removed.

---

## 3. Difference From The Previous BHOPE Offer

The earlier BHOPE offer was a discounted access model:

- Regular Muslim Will price: $350 CAD
- BHOPE partner access: $300 CAD
- Member savings: $50 CAD
- Limited pilot: 100 family spots

The new idea is a sponsored legacy model:

- BHOPE pays or sponsors the will package
- The member pays $0, subject to final commercial approval
- The member includes a charitable bequest to BHOPE
- BHOPE builds a future legacy-giving pipeline
- The campaign becomes easier to promote because the user-facing offer is free

The old model says:

**"Save $50 on your will."**

The new model says:

**"BHOPE will sponsor your will so you can protect your family and leave a future charitable legacy."**

That is a much stronger community and donor-development message.

---

## 4. Strategic Logic

This model works because it aligns three interests.

### For the family

The family receives practical support to prepare a will, document important decisions, and reduce uncertainty for loved ones.

### For BHOPE

BHOPE creates a structured legacy-giving channel. Rather than only asking for donations now, BHOPE helps supporters document future charitable intentions in their wills.

### For Muslim Will

Muslim Will gains a high-conversion partner channel because the offer removes price friction and comes through a trusted community organization.

The strongest business logic is:

**The sponsored cost is a present-day investment in future charitable bequests.**

## 5. Recommended Positioning

The offer should be positioned as a family-first legacy program, not as a charity-first fundraising tactic.

Recommended positioning:

**Protect your family today. Leave a legacy of hope tomorrow.**

Alternative positioning:

**BHOPE is sponsoring Muslim Will packages to help families prepare clearly and include a future charitable legacy with intention.**

Avoid positioning that makes the charitable bequest feel like pressure or payment.

Do not frame it as:

- "Get a free will in exchange for donating to BHOPE."
- "BHOPE buys your will if you give 5%."
- "Guaranteed future return for BHOPE."
- "100% Sharia-compliant."
- "Legally guaranteed."

The safer frame is:

**BHOPE is removing the cost barrier so families can complete responsible planning and, if they choose or agree under the program terms, document a charitable bequest within applicable legal and Islamic limits.**

Safer version if the bequest is required for sponsorship:

The final version depends on whether the BHOPE bequest is voluntary, strongly encouraged, or required as a sponsorship condition.

## 8. Bequest Structure

The HTML proposes:

- Minimum bequest: 5% of net worth
- Beneficiary: BHOPE
- Program choice:
  - Healing Hands
  - Bright Futures
  - Hope in Crisis

This is a powerful structure because it lets the supporter connect their future gift to a meaningful cause.

However, the 5% minimum must be handled carefully.

Questions to confirm:

- Is 5% mandatory or only recommended?
- Is 5% calculated from net estate, net worth, or estate residue?
- Can the donor choose a fixed amount instead of a percentage?
- Can the donor choose more than 5%?
- Can the donor split the bequest across BHOPE programs?
- What happens if the donor later changes the will?
- Is the sponsorship still valid if the donor removes BHOPE later?
- How will BHOPE track intended bequests without overstepping privacy boundaries?

Recommended safer wording:

**Participants may include a charitable bequest to BHOPE, such as 5% of their estate, within the permissible bequest portion and subject to applicable legal and Islamic limits.**

If BHOPE wants a required minimum, the wording should be reviewed carefully by legal and religious advisors before public use.

## 9. User Journey

The HTML suggests a seven-step journey:

1. Create the will through the Muslim Will platform.
2. Dedicate a charitable bequest to BHOPE.
3. Submit the application.
4. Complete Islamic review.
5. Complete legal review.
6. Attend an attestation or signing session.
7. Receive the completed document package.

Recommended refined journey:

1. Check eligibility for the BHOPE-sponsored place.
2. Start the Muslim Will intake.
3. Enter family, executor, guardian, asset, and final-wishes information.
4. Choose whether and how to include a charitable bequest to BHOPE, according to the program rules.
5. Submit the intake for review.
6. Complete document review and any required clarifications.
7. Complete Islamic inheritance review and legal preparation.
8. Attend the signing or attestation session.
9. Receive final documents and storage/signing guidance.
10. Receive future review reminders.

The user journey should feel supportive, not transactional.

## 10. "Mithaq" Package Concept

The HTML introduces the name "Mithaq" for the completed legacy package.

It describes the package as including:

- Last Will & Testament
- Power of Attorney for Property
- Power of Attorney for Personal Care

This is a potentially strong product name because "Mithaq" suggests covenant, seriousness, and trust.

However, this needs confirmation before use:

- Are Power of Attorney documents always included?
- Are they included in the sponsored package price?
- Are they available in every province/jurisdiction being targeted?
- Is "Mithaq" already approved as product language by Muslim Will?
- Will users understand the word without explanation?

Recommended use:

Use "Mithaq" only if the package contents are confirmed. Otherwise, use simpler wording:

**Your completed Muslim Will package**

or

**Your will and supporting documents, where applicable**

**100% Sharia Compliant**

**Designed to reflect Islamic inheritance principles**

Avoid:

**Legally valid**

Replace with:

**Prepared with attention to applicable legal requirements and proper signing steps**

or, if Ontario-specific and legally approved:

**Designed to be legally valid under Ontario law when properly signed and witnessed**

Avoid:

**Islamically certified**

Replace with:

**Reviewed for Islamic inheritance considerations by qualified reviewers**

Avoid:

**Guaranteed future charitable impact**

Replace with:

**Potential future charitable legacy**

Avoid using unverified numbers:

- 1000+ wills sponsored
- 10,000+ wills created
- 100% Sharia compliant

These should be verified or removed.

## 16. Compliance And Ethical Notes

This model is powerful, but it needs careful boundaries.

Key concerns:

- A free will in exchange for a required bequest may need legal review.
- The donor must understand whether the bequest is voluntary or a condition of sponsorship.
- The donor should not feel pressured to choose charity over family.
- The bequest should stay within applicable legal and Islamic limits.
- BHOPE should not provide legal advice, religious rulings, or will-drafting services.
- Muslim Will should avoid guaranteeing legal validity or religious compliance in every case.
- Data-sharing between Muslim Will and BHOPE must be clear, consent-based, and privacy-compliant.
- Any reporting to BHOPE should avoid exposing sensitive personal estate details unless explicit consent exists.

Recommended compliance note:

**BHOPE is sponsoring access as a community benefit. Muslim Will provides the guided will-planning pathway. BHOPE does not provide legal advice, religious rulings, or will-drafting services. Charitable bequests are subject to applicable legal and Islamic limits, and legal validity depends on accurate information, appropriate review, and proper signing and witnessing.**
