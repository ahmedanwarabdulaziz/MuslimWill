Why It Matters

A Waseyyah is not just
wisdom — it is worship.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "It is not permissible for any Muslim who has something to bequeath to sleep for two nights without having a written will." Writing your will is an act of faith, care, and responsibility.

01

A Religious Obligation

Islamic scholars across all madhabs affirm that writing a will is highly recommended — or obligatory — for any Muslim with assets or dependants.

02

Protection for Your Family

Without a will, your estate may be distributed under intestacy law — which does not align with Islamic inheritance rules and can leave loved ones unprotected.

03

Sadaqah Jariyah

A bequest is one of the most powerful forms of ongoing charity. Your 5% dedication continues to benefit others — and earn reward — long after you are gone.

04

Peace of Mind

Knowing your affairs are in order — Islamically and legally — is a profound relief for you and your family. It takes less than 30 minutes to get started.
