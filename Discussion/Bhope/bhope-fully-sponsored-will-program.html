How it works

A clear path from eligibility to completion.

Every step should feel simple, guided, and professional. The goal is to help you move from intention to a completed will package without confusion.

Step 1

Use your existing BHOPE access or become a BHOPE member before starting the sponsored pathway.

Step 2

Enter family details, executor choices, guardianship preferences, assets, and final wishes.

Step 3
