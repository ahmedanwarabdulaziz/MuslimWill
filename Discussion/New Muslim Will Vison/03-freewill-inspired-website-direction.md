# FreeWill Inspired Website Direction

## What FreeWill is doing well

After reviewing `https://www.freewill.com/`, the strongest reusable patterns are structural, not stylistic.

### 1. Clear category architecture

FreeWill immediately shows users that the company serves different needs through a clean top-level structure.

### 2. One obvious primary action

The site keeps returning to one main CTA and does not make the page feel overloaded.

### 3. Fast credibility

The homepage uses:

- press logos
- review signals
- big usage numbers
- legal disclaimers

This creates trust quickly.

### 4. Product-card clarity

Users can understand the offer in scan mode.
That matters for Patriva because umbrella brands can become confusing if the services are not visually separated.

### 5. Simple process explanation

The process is short, concrete, and non-intimidating.

## What we should borrow

- simple navigation logic
- clean service segmentation
- visible proof points
- concise process steps
- calm educational tone
- clear professional-boundary language

## What we should not borrow directly

- the "free" positioning
- broad secular estate-planning language
- heavy emphasis on individual legal-document utility over faith-and-family meaning
- a nonprofit-driven message architecture

Patriva needs warmth, dignity, and community trust in a way that is more relational than FreeWill.

## Better adaptation for Patriva

### Homepage flow

1. Hero:
   Patriva supports Muslim families at every milestone.

2. Milestone strip:
   engagement, marriage, new child, home, family agreements, estate planning, farewell.

3. Service cards:
   Muslim Will, Mizan, Sakina, Ikram.

4. Why families trust this:
   legal care, Islamic grounding, community awareness, coordinated workflow.

5. Spotlight section:
   keep Muslim Will as the strongest immediate conversion block.

6. Cross-service explanation:
   one family, multiple documents, one coordinated path.

7. FAQ and boundary note.

8. Final CTA:
   start with your will or speak with us.

- Sakina
- Ikram

- About

## Visual direction to translate

The useful part of the FreeWill reference is:

- clean whitespace
- clear blocks
- calm confidence
- obvious next steps

The visual language should still stay aligned with this project's existing trust cues:

- deep green
- cream
- disciplined gold accents
- faith-conscious warmth

## Strategic bottom line

Use FreeWill as a benchmark for:

- clarity
- conversion flow
- service architecture

Do not use it as a brand identity template.

The winning version for this project is:

- FreeWill's clarity
- plus Patriva's milestone story
- plus Muslim Will's trust and domain authority
