# Muslim Will Homepage Direction

## Working context

This file is the direct working draft for the `Muslim Will` homepage.

It uses:

- `https://www.freewill.com/` as a structural guide
- `Patriva` as the umbrella-brand context
- `Muslim Will` as the focused product and conversion site

Reviewed against the FreeWill homepage on `2026-04-22`.

## Core principle

We are not copying FreeWill.

We are borrowing the parts that make it effective:

- clarity
- simple scanning
- one obvious CTA
- fast trust-building
- clear process explanation
- visible legal boundaries

But the message and tone must stay ours:

- faith-conscious
- family-first
- premium and calm
- Canada-first
- legally careful

## What this homepage must do

The Muslim Will homepage should do five jobs:

1. explain the offer fast
2. build trust within seconds
3. show the process simply
4. make the service feel responsible and guided
5. move users toward `Start Your Will`

## What the homepage should not do

- It should not explain all of Patriva in depth.
- It should not try to launch all four brands equally.
- It should not become a corporate umbrella homepage.
- It should not lose the strong SEO focus around Islamic wills.

## Positioning statement

`Muslim Will is the guided Islamic will-planning pathway for Muslim families in Canada, and part of the wider Patriva family-services vision.`

## 4. Why people begin

- why this matters now
- why families delay
- why clarity is valuable

`For many Muslim families, preparing a will is not rejected. It is delayed.`

### Supporting copy

`The challenge is often not belief in the importance of a will. It is uncertainty about where to begin. A guided pathway helps families move from intention to responsible action with more clarity and less hesitation.`

`Protect the people who depend on you`

## 5. What you can do with a will

This is a strong FreeWill pattern worth adapting.

FreeWill clearly shows what the product actually helps users do.
Muslim Will should do the same, but with a more focused and faith-conscious lens.

### H2

`What this helps you protect`

### Suggested list

- name guardians for minor children
- document family responsibilities clearly
- organize bequest intentions
- make room for charitable giving
- reduce uncertainty around final wishes

### Section note

This should stay practical and easy to scan.
It should not become a legal lecture.

## 6. How it works

This must be simple, concrete, and confidence-building.

FreeWill does this well by making the process feel manageable.
We should do the same.

### H2

`A guided pathway from family details to final execution.`

### Step 1

`Start with guided intake`

Enter your key family, guardian, and bequest information through a structured process.

### Step 2

`Organize your planning clearly`

Your information is structured around the details needed for a faith-conscious and legally careful will-preparation process.

### Step 3

`Move through legal review`

The will moves through the proper legal workflow and document-preparation process.

### Step 4

`Review, sign, and complete`

You review your document and complete signing and witnessing correctly.

### Process note

`Legal validity depends on accurate information, proper review, and correct signing and witnessing.`

## 7. Legal and Islamic accountability

This is one of the strongest existing Muslim Will themes and should stay.

### H2

`A platform with clear professional boundaries.`

### Three accountability blocks

#### The platform

Muslim Will guides workflow, user input, document coordination, and process clarity.

#### Islamic inheritance awareness

Islamic inheritance review is handled by qualified Islamic scholars who review the inheritance structure for Islamic inheritance considerations. This review supports faith-conscious planning and does not replace a personal fatwa for complex individual circumstances.

#### Legal review

Independent legal professionals handle the legal review and execution-related requirements within the actual legal workflow.

### Boundary note

`Muslim Will is not a law firm and does not issue legal advice or fatwas.`

This sentence should stay visible and clear.

## Copy guidance

### We should sound like

- calm
- premium
- clear
- dignified
- faith-conscious
- careful with legal wording

### We should not sound like

- sales-heavy
- fear-based
- generic legal marketing
- overly corporate
- vague and poetic without clarity

## What we are borrowing from FreeWill

- strong hero clarity
- visible CTA priority
- easy-to-scan content blocks
- practical feature framing
- short process explanation
- clear legal disclaimer logic

## What we are not borrowing from FreeWill

- `free` positioning
- mainstream secular copy
- broad product-directory homepage structure
- U.S.-state-based framing
- nonprofit-led donation narrative

## SEO guidance

The page should still target:

- `Islamic will Canada`
- `Muslim will Canada`
- `Islamic will Ontario`
- `Shariah compliant will Canada`

The Patriva layer should support trust, not dilute search intent.

## Final strategic rule

The Muslim Will homepage should feel like:

- a focused will-planning website first
- a Patriva-backed brand second

That order matters.

If reversed, we risk losing clarity and conversion.
