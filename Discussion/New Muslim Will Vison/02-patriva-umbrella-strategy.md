# Patriva Umbrella Strategy

## The actual business change

This is not a small brand refresh.

It is a shift from:

- a single-category offer: `Muslim Will`

to:

- a family-services group: `Patriva`

That means the website has to answer a different first question.

### Old first question

- "Why should I trust Muslim Will for Islamic estate planning?"

### New first question

- "Why should I trust Patriva to support my family across important life milestones?"

Muslim Will still matters, but it becomes one strong proof of the bigger platform.

## The master-brand line

The strongest line in the Patriva framework is:

- `With you at every milestone. Not just the difficult ones.`

That line changes everything about the homepage story.

## Brand architecture

### Parent brand

- `Patriva`
- role: master brand and trust container

### Service brands

- `Muslim Will`
- `Mizan`
- `Sakina`
- `Ikram`

## What this means for the website

The homepage should no longer open with death-adjacent planning language alone.

It should open with protection across family milestones such as:

- marriage
- a new child
- a home purchase
- family agreements
- estate planning
- farewell preparation

Then it should show where Muslim Will fits inside that journey.

## Recommended positioning model

### Patriva homepage

Purpose:

- explain the umbrella
- show the milestone map
- introduce the service family
- move users into the right service

### Muslim Will page

Purpose:

- stay focused on wills
- preserve conversion clarity
- remain the strongest immediate revenue path

## Best phase-1 rollout

If we want a realistic live test without overbuilding, the best move is:

1. keep the current domain experience centered on Muslim Will
2. add visible parent-brand context for Patriva
3. add a milestone-based section that introduces the wider ecosystem
4. treat full Patriva multi-brand navigation as phase 2

## Why this phased approach is safer

- It protects current conversion clarity.
- It lets us test whether Patriva increases trust or creates confusion.
- It avoids launching three additional brands before the audience understands them.
- It keeps Muslim Will as the immediate commercial engine.

## Message hierarchy for phase 1

1. `Protect your family with clarity.`
2. `Muslim Will is part of Patriva.`
3. `Patriva supports Muslim families across every milestone.`
4. `Today, start with your will.`

## What not to do

- Do not bury Muslim Will under four equal products on day one.
- Do not copy FreeWill's exact consumer promise because FreeWill is free and broad-market.
- Do not make Patriva feel abstract or corporate.
- Do not introduce all brands with equal visual weight before their offers are explained.

## Strategic bottom line

The website should evolve from:

- a premium Islamic will website

into:

- the first visible doorway into the Patriva ecosystem

That is the clearest way to honor the business owner's new direction without sacrificing conversion.
