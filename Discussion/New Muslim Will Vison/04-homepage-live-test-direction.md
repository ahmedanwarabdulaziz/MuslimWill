# Homepage Live Test Direction

## Goal

Test the new Patriva-led story on the live Muslim Will website without rebuilding the whole platform first.

## Recommended test model

Keep the current website codebase, but change the homepage story in a staged way.

## Homepage structure for the test

### 1. Hero

Suggested direction:

- eyebrow: `Part of Patriva`
- H1: `With your family at every milestone. Start by protecting what matters most.`
- supporting copy:
  Patriva helps Muslim families prepare for the milestones that shape family life. Muslim Will is the estate-planning pathway within that system, built to help you document your wishes with legal care and Islamic clarity.

Primary CTA:

- `Start Your Will`

Secondary CTA:

- `See How It Works`

### 2. Patriva introduction band

Short message:

- `Muslim Will is part of Patriva, a family-services group built for Muslim families across life's important milestones.`

### 3. Milestone map section

Cards or timeline:

- Marriage and agreements
- New child and guardianship
- Home and family protection
- Estate planning
- Farewell preparation

This is the moment where the site tells the new business story.

### 4. Muslim Will spotlight

Keep a dedicated section that says clearly:

- what Muslim Will is
- who it is for
- why it is the best first step

This protects conversion focus.

### 5. Trust and accountability

Keep and refine the current legal and Islamic boundary section.
It is still one of the strongest trust assets in the project.

### 6. Cross-service teaser

Simple cards:

- `Muslim Will`
- `Mizan`
- `Sakina`
- `Ikram`

Each card should explain the service in one line only.

### 7. Final CTA

Use a clear decision:

- `Start Your Will`
- `Book a Call`

## What should change first in code

If we build this live next, the first code changes should be:

1. repair broken navigation and CTA destinations
2. add Patriva parent-brand signaling to header, hero, and footer
3. replace the current will-only homepage opening with milestone-based framing
4. add a light service-portfolio section without overcomplicating the navigation

## Why this is the right test

- It is visible enough to validate the new strategy.
- It does not require launching all four brands at once.
- It preserves the current strongest conversion path.
- It helps us learn whether Patriva increases trust, curiosity, or confusion.

## Success questions for the live test

- Do users better understand who is behind Muslim Will?
- Does Patriva increase credibility or dilute clarity?
- Do more users continue into the will flow?
- Do more users click to learn about the wider ecosystem?

## Recommendation

The best next implementation is not a full rebrand everywhere.

It is a controlled homepage test:

- `Muslim Will` remains the offer
- `Patriva` becomes the visible umbrella
- the website starts telling the bigger story
