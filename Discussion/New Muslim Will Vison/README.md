# New Muslim Will Vison

This folder is the working space for the new strategic direction where `Patriva` becomes the umbrella brand and `Muslim Will` becomes one core service inside that family platform.

## Why this folder exists

The business change is bigger than a homepage redesign.

We are not only changing the visual direction.
We are changing the story:

- from a single-product estate-planning brand
- to an umbrella family-services brand
- with Muslim Will positioned inside a broader milestone-based ecosystem

## Core conclusion

The new direction should not be:

- "make Muslim Will look more like FreeWill"

It should be:

- "use FreeWill's clarity and conversion structure"
- "while repositioning the business under Patriva"
- "and deciding how much of that change we want to expose on the live site first"

## Recommended next move

Start with a staged live test:

1. keep the current domain and codebase
2. introduce Patriva visibly as the parent brand
3. reframe the homepage around family milestones, not only wills
4. keep Muslim Will as the main conversion path for phase 1

That gives us a real market test without forcing a full replatform first.
