## Purpose

This is the entry point for the My Vault section. It exists because 4 feature pages (`/vault/obligations-register`, `/vault/estate-inventory`, `/vault/sealed-disclosures`, `/vault/final-words`) all link here but no page existed — resulting in a 404.

The page does three things:
1. Explains what The Vault is and why it matters
2. Presents all 4 Vault branches clearly so the user knows which one to enter
3. Explains how executor access works (the release process) and the privacy model

This is not a product sales page — it is an orientation hub. The user arriving here is either already a subscriber exploring their Vault, or a prospect who clicked a "See What's in the Vault" CTA from a feature page.

### Section 3 — How the release process works

**Section label:** The Release Process

**H2:**
> Your Vault is sealed until the moment it needs to open.

**3-step process (numbered):**

**Step 1 — While you are alive**
> Only you have access to your Vault. No one — not family, not our team — can view what you have stored. You can update it any time.

**Step 2 — After your passing is verified**
> Your executor submits an official verification through Muslim Will. This triggers the release process. We verify the claim before any access is granted.

**Step 3 — Access is granted with precision**
> Each item in your Vault is assigned to a specific recipient. Your executor receives what they need to administer the estate. Sealed Disclosures are delivered to the individuals you named. Every access event is logged in a full audit trail.

**Closing line:**
> You do not lose control. You extend it — beyond your lifetime.
