## Purpose

This is the entry point for the My Bridge section — mirroring what `/vault` does for the Vault section. It presents all 4 document types in one place, explains what each one is, and routes the visitor to the right page.

**Important positioning note:** Not all 4 documents are included in The Bridge subscription:
- ✅ **Last Will & Testament** — included in The Bridge ($5.99/month)
- ✅ **Charitable Legacy** — part of the will, included in The Bridge
- ➕ **POA for Personal Care** — add-on ($99) or Full Package ($350)
- ➕ **POA for Property** — add-on ($99) or Full Package ($350)

The page must present all 4 without making The Bridge feel incomplete. POAs are framed as "for specific situations" — not as something missing from the base subscription.

The audience here is either:
- A prospect researching what will documents they need
- A subscriber exploring their documents section

## Tone Notes

- **The Bridge is the star.** The will and charitable legacy lead — they are included and complete. POAs follow as situational additions.
- **No "upgrade" language.** The POA framing section explains when they're relevant, not that they're necessary. The word "upgrade" should never appear.
- **The POA badges say "Available as add-on" — not "Not included."** The distinction matters. "Available as add-on" is additive. "Not included" is subtractive.
- **Short and scannable.** Like the Vault hub, this page orients — it doesn't deep-dive. Each document has its own full page for that.
