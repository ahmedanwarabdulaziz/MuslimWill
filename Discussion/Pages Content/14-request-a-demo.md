# Page: Request a Demo

**URL:** `/community/request-demo`
**Nav location:** Community & Giving CTA / Professionals CTA
**Eyebrow on page:** Partner With Us

---

## Page Role

This is the conversion page for all Community & Giving and Professionals partnerships. Anyone who clicks "Request a Demo" from any dropdown or page lands here. It should feel warm, clear, and low-friction — not like a sales form, but like reaching out to a team that is genuinely ready to help.

**Primary CTA:** Submit the contact form

---

## Audience

An organisation leader, charity director, mosque representative, financial advisor, lawyer, or any professional who has decided they want to learn more about working with The Muslim Will. They are ready to talk — they just need a frictionless way to do it.

---

## H1

> Let's talk about what we can build for your community.

**Supporting line under H1:**
> Every partnership is shaped around the organisation, its community, and its goals. The best place to start is a conversation — tell us who you are and what you are trying to do, and we will come prepared.

---

## Section 1 — What Happens After You Request a Demo

**H2:** A real conversation. No sales pressure.

**Three steps:**

**1. Tell us about yourself**
Fill in the short form below — your name, your organisation, and what you are interested in. Takes less than two minutes.

**2. We will be in touch within one business day**
Our partnerships team reviews every request personally. We will reach out to schedule a time that works for you — a call, a video walkthrough, or a visit if you are in the area.

**3. We come prepared**
We will already know who you are and what you do. The conversation will be specific to your organisation — not a generic product pitch.
