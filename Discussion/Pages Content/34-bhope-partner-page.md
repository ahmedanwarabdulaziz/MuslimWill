## Tone Notes

- **The page is a gift, not a sales pitch.** BHope invested in their members — the tone should feel like an announcement of a benefit, not a promotional campaign.
- **Islamic framing is present and purposeful.** The Hadith in Section 3 is used once. The Sadaqah Jariyah framing is the emotional and spiritual core.
- **BHope's voice in Section 4 should feel authentic** — not like marketing copy. It should sound like a statement from a charity that genuinely believes in what it's doing.
- **The charity section (Section 6) is confident, not desperate.** "This is the model" — show, don't sell.
- **Link to bhope.ca throughout.** This is a partnership — always refer people back to BHope for their code and their story.
