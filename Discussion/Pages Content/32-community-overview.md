## Purpose

This is the entry point for the Community & Giving section. Multiple pages (including `/charitable-legacy`) link to `/community-and-giving` which doesn't exist. This page resolves that and gives the section a proper home.

The audience here is different from the individual/family pages. These are:
- Imams and mosque administrators
- Islamic charity and nonprofit leaders
- Donors who want to include a cause in their will
- Community organizers looking to bring estate planning to their congregation

The page explains what The Muslim Will offers to organizations and communities — and routes each visitor to the right programme.

This is not a sales page in the traditional sense. It is a directory page with warmth — it explains the vision, then presents the programmes clearly so each visitor self-selects.

> The Prophet ﷺ said it is not permissible for a Muslim who has something to bequeath to sleep two nights without a written will. > When a Muslim passes away without a will, the consequences fall on the community. Families seek guidance from the imam. Disputes arise between heirs. Debts go unresolved. Children's guardianship goes undocumented. Community organisations lose years of planned donations. The Muslim Will gives your organisation the tools to change this — before it becomes a crisis.

## Tone Notes

- **Audience is professional, not individual.** Write to an imam or a charity CEO — not to a family starting their will.
- **Islamic framing is present and purposeful.** This audience will respond to the Hadith and the community obligation framing. Use it once, well.
- **No pricing on this page.** Community programmes are discussed personally. No rates, no tables.
- **Warm but direct.** These are community leaders — they are busy and they need to self-select quickly. The cards should be scannable.
- **The request-demo CTA is soft.** "Request a conversation" not "Book a demo" — community organisations respond better to relational language.

---

## Design Notes

- The 5 programme cards should be in a clean grid (2+2+1 or 3+2 layout)
- Each card needs a clear "who it's for" label — this helps visitors self-select fast
- Section 3 (Islamic case) should feel like a thoughtful editorial block, not a feature list — no bullet points
- Section 5 (individual redirect) should be visually quiet — small text, not a full section — just a gentle "wrong door?" nudge at the bottom
