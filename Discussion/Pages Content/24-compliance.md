---

## Section 2 — The Three Layers of Our Process

**H2:** Each layer has a defined role. No layer overreaches into another.

**Three cards:**

**The Platform**
The Muslim Will manages the intake workflow, Faraid calculations, document generation, secure Vault storage, and the coordination between the client and the professional review layers. The platform does not provide legal advice or issue religious rulings.

**Islamic Inheritance Review**
Qualified Islamic scholars within our network review inheritance calculations for Islamic correctness — verifying Faraid shares and identifying complex situations requiring additional guidance. This review supports faith-conscious planning. It is not a personal fatwa and does not replace a scholarly consultation for complex individual circumstances.

**Legal Workflow and Document Review**
Licensed legal professionals review and oversee the document structure and execution requirements within the Ontario legal framework. Documents are prepared to meet provincial requirements for a valid testamentary instrument. This is not independent legal advice — clients with complex legal situations are encouraged to seek independent counsel.

## Section 4 — What Legal Validity Depends On

**H2:** A clear statement on enforceability

**Copy:**
> Legal validity of a will in Ontario depends on accurate information provided by the client, correct completion of the document, proper signing, and correct witnessing in accordance with provincial requirements. The Muslim Will is structured to support all of these steps — but the client's responsibility for accuracy and the correct execution of the document remains essential.

> We state this not to diminish confidence in the process — but because honest communication about scope is part of how a trustworthy platform operates.

## Section 5 — What We Are Not

**H2:** Boundaries stated plainly

- Muslim Will is **not a law firm** and does not provide legal advice
- Muslim Will is **not an Islamic jurisprudence body** and does not issue fatwas
- Muslim Will **does not guarantee legal validity** in every case — validity depends on accurate information and correct execution
- Muslim Will **does not replace a lawyer** for complex estate situations
- Muslim Will **does not replace a personal scholarly consultation** for complex religious questions
