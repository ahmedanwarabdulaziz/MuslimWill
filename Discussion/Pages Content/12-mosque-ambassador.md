## Section 1 — What the Mosque Ambassador Programme Includes

**H2:** Everything your community needs. Nothing your team has to manage.

**Three programme elements:**

### A Co-Branded Will Planning Page
Your congregation accesses The Muslim Will through a dedicated page that carries your masjid's name and identity. They arrive through a trusted gateway — your community, your name — and receive a complete, guided Islamic estate planning experience on the other side.

### Outreach and Awareness Materials
We provide ready-to-use materials to help you introduce Islamic estate planning to your congregation — Friday khutbah outlines, community announcement templates, social media content, and talking points for your leadership team. You introduce the conversation in a way that feels natural to your community. We provide the words.

### A Community Dashboard
Track uptake within your congregation — how many families have started their estate plans, how many have completed them, and the overall impact of the programme on the community you serve. No personal data is shared — only aggregate numbers to help you understand the difference you are making.
